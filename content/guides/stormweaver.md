      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Stormweaver**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Stormweaver** is a sorceress ascendancy, so we start on the spell side of the tree with a lot of spell damage and energy shield. The **Crown of Eyes** makes increases to spell damage apply to attacks as well, which turns all of that spell damage into werewolf damage. Rage works twice for us. It gives attack damage, and **Mystical Rage** makes every rage also grant increased spell damage, which the **Crown of Eyes** converts to attack damage again. **Berserk** amplifies all of it, and I run 67 maximum rage.

      The ascendancy adds two things on top of that. **Shaper of Storms** makes all of our damage contribute to shock chance, and **Strike Twice** lets a target carry two of our shocks at once, so we shock everything and shock it twice. The second thing is mana. The werewolf is mana hungry, and **Constant Gale** gives us a permanent **Arcane Surge**, which is a lot of mana regeneration.

      The defence is energy shield. I take **Chaos Inoculation** and stack as much energy shield as I can, 11k in my case, and **Force of Will** makes part of our damage come out of mana before life, which the mana regeneration sustains. **Shavronne’s Satchel** applies our life flask to energy shield, so we also get burst recovery on demand.

      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups), and **Shred** and **Cross Slash** use their standard setups unchanged. Everything below is what the **Stormweaver** changes.

      **Lunar Assault**. I stay on **Ice Bite II**, because every freeze gives us extra cold damage and we freeze constantly. **Bhatair’s Vengeance** is better with a lot of rage, but at 67 maximum rage it isn’t worth it here. **Rigwald’s Ferocity** trades attack speed for damage, or the reverse, depending on the weapon set, so I keep **Lunar Assault** in the second weapon set to get the damage.

      **Pounce**. **Brutus’ Brain** keeps the wolves from dying, and they feed both **Predator’s Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**, so we want as many as possible. They deal no damage, but **Culling Strike II** still works through them. **Charged Mark** has a second use here. Detonating the mark leaves shocked ground that applies one shock, and our hits apply the second one thanks to **Strike Twice**. **Mark for Death II** breaks the target’s armour as we hit it, which is very good on bosses.

      **Arctic Howl**. **Living Lightning II** triggers whenever the supported skill deals lightning damage, and **Arctic Howl** deals a little in this build, which is all it needs. The spirits deal no real damage, but they count as minions in our presence, so they raise the damage from **Predator’s Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**.

      **Lunar Blessing**. We can’t use **Atalui’s Bloodletting**, because we have no life to pay for it. **Efficiency II** takes its place, since **Lunar Blessing** is very mana hungry. **Vorana’s Siege** is only for damage and area, so without it you can use **Magnified Area II** or **Cold Mastery** for an extra level.

      **Herald of Ice**. I get **Dangerous Blossom** from my **Megalomaniac**, which gives 10% of damage as extra physical damage and lets me run the armour break setup from the general guide. If you don’t have **Dangerous Blossom**, skip the armour break and use the standard setup with **Elemental Focus**, **Magnified Area II**, and **Cold Mastery**.

      **Eternal Rage**. **Herbalism II** increases life recovery from flasks, so the flask we heal our energy shield with recovers more. I fit **Clarity I** with the 10 spirit I had left over, since the wolf is mana hungry.

      **Berserk**. This one is essential, because it amplifies the spell damage from **Mystical Rage** as well. **Dialla’s Desire** improves it further and is not needed. **Uhtred’s Omen** only grants extra levels when exactly one other support is used with it, so if you don’t have **Dialla’s Desire**, put any support in its place, such as **Armour Demolisher II**, even if it does nothing for you.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[stormweaver-full-endgame]

      **Weapon**. A **Maji Talisman**, for the maximum rage. Besides that you want as much damage as you can get: physical and fire damage, attack speed, and levels of attack skills. Attributes are always welcome too, because the werewolf juggles strength, intelligence, and a little dexterity, so intelligence or strength on the weapon helps. **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** gives 5% of damage as extra damage of all elements, and you will always want it. **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony** gives 13% of damage as extra chaos damage. I like it, but any other rune works.

      **Helmet**. The **Crown of Eyes**. It makes the whole build work, and I would not play it without one. You need level 45 to equip it, and that matters for leveling. Leveling a **Stormweaver** as a werewolf from the sorceress side without the **Crown of Eyes** is painful, so I don’t recommend it. Level with spells and swap to the werewolf in the late game. If you are stubborn you can level as a werewolf, but it will be a struggle.

      **Amulet**. Spirit, maximum energy shield, levels of melee skills, and all elemental resistances to fix your resistances. Increased energy shield from equipped body armour is a lot of energy shield with a good body armour, but it is a desecrated modifier and not required. Mine is anointed with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket. A good **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage** can be an upgrade over a rare amulet like mine, since it frees up 100 spirit. It has one prefix and one suffix fewer, so it needs to be rolled correctly with energy shield, and you then have to fix your resistances on your other gear pieces.

      **Body armour**. A rare **Vile Robe**. We want all the energy shield we can get, plus spirit and resistances.

      **Belt**. The belt is a very competitive slot. You can solve your resistances with it, or run a **Headhunter**, which is good in this build, so use one if you have it. I use **Shavronne’s Satchel**, which makes life flask recovery also apply to energy shield. Solve your resistances with the other pieces so the belt doesn’t have to.

      The flask matters. You need a life flask with instant recovery, no exceptions. A life flask only works if you have life to recover, even when you have energy shield to recover. With **Chaos Inoculation** we sit at one life all the time, so a normal flask will never heal our energy shield. With instant recovery, every time you have energy shield to recover you press the flask and get a burst of it immediately.

      **Gloves**. **Sine Aequo**, as on every werewolf build. It makes us immobilise enemies at 50% buildup instead of 100%, so I never worry about freeze buildup, which other builds have to invest in. Rare gloves give more damage, but only switch once you have enough damage to make up for the freeze buildup you lose. A **Cadigan’s Epiphany** in the socket gives the gloves a jewel socket, which I think is the best thing you can put in gloves. In it I want spell damage, which the **Crown of Eyes** converts to attack damage, maximum energy shield, and skill speed while shapeshifted. If you are rich, a better jewel with other modifiers also works.

      **Boots**. Energy shield above all, on a full energy shield base like **Sekhema Sandals**. You can even sacrifice movement speed, since the werewolf has plenty. Boots with 20% movement speed are fine. I have movement speed, energy shield, resistances, and a little energy shield recharge rate to be on the safe side.

      **Rings**. Flat damage on both, for attacks, plus resistances and attributes. You also need one source of flat life regeneration per second, and it can be on any piece: a ring, the amulet, the body armour. **Berserk** makes us lose life per second while we have rage, and we have one life. Without life regeneration you will die out of nowhere and not understand what is killing you. I made this mistake myself. One life means a small amount is enough. Mine has 3 on a ring and my total life recovery is 5 per second, which keeps **Berserk** from killing me. It has to be flat, because percentage life regeneration, the **Vitality** support, life percentage on a jewel, and life percentage on the tree do nothing for a life pool of 1.

      **Flasks and charms**. Bring a good mana flask. Mapping is fine on regeneration, but in a single target boss fight where you freeze with **Lunar Assault** you can run out of mana, and a flask fixes that. For charms I like a **Dousing Charm**, because energy shield characters seem to suffer more from ignites. A **Thawing Charm** is a must, and the unique **Nascent Hope** is very good if you can get it. I use **The Fall of the Axe** on a **Silver Charm** to be free from slows. I don’t use it for the onslaught, since **Savage Fury** already gives that, so a normal **Silver Charm** works too.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      ::tree-image[stormweaver]

      On the ascendancy I take **Shaper of Storms**, **Strike Twice**, **Constant Gale**, **Force of Will**, and the shock chance and mana regeneration nodes. **Shaper of Storms** makes all damage from hits contribute to shock chance. **Strike Twice** lets two of our shocks apply at once, at the cost of 25% less shock magnitude. **Constant Gale** gives us **Arcane Surge** permanently, which is 15% increased cast speed and 20% more mana regeneration rate. We don’t cast, so the regeneration is the point. **Force of Will** makes 20% of damage be taken from mana before life, and it increases the effect of **Arcane Surge** for every 10% of mana we are missing.

      For energy shield I take **Pure Energy**, **Melding**, **Insightfulness**, **Dampening Shield**, **Patient Barrier**, and **Enhanced Barrier**, and I like having a lot of it. Do you need 11k? No, 6k does the job, but at 11k the biggest slam of a boss doesn’t kill me. For spell damage, which the **Crown of Eyes** turns into attack damage, I take **Raw Power**, **Potent Incantation**, and **Mystical Rage**, plus **Open Mind** for mana regeneration. **Arcane Nature** is only this good because of **Constant Gale**. It gives 30% increased spell damage and 12% increased area of effect while we have **Arcane Surge**, which is always. **Endless Blizzard** gives plus 1 to the level of all cold skills, so **Herald of Ice**, **Lunar Blessing**, **Lunar Assault**, and **Shred** all benefit. **Eternal Rage** and **Berserk** don’t.

      **Heavy Frost** is a must, as on all my werewolf builds. **Thin Ice** goes with it. It gives 50% increased damage with hits against frozen enemies and more freeze buildup, and we freeze everything.

      **Chaos Inoculation** needs one more thing. Monsters can stun us in a chain and stun lock us in place, and stun threshold is based on maximum life, so at one life we have none. **Abasement** gives stun threshold equal to 30% of maximum energy shield. You need it, so please take it. **Dampening Shield** adds to it, and if you still have problems with stuns, a **Stone Charm** makes you immune.

      I socket **From Nothing** on **Pain Attunement**, which lets me allocate the nodes in its radius without a connected path. That gets me **Mystical Rage**, which is fundamental to the build, **Pack Encouragement** for 5% increased attack damage per minion in our presence, and **Desensitisation**, which gives 25% increased critical damage bonus and reduces the critical damage bonus of hits against us. It also gets me **Bringer of Order** for increased damage and **Hide of the Bear** for armour and stun threshold while shapeshifted, without paying for the nodes around them, which I don’t want. If you don’t have a **From Nothing**, path to **Mystical Rage**, because the build needs it.

      The **Heroic Tragedy** with the line of Vorana converts a keystone in its radius into **Black Scythe Training**. We gain no inherent bonus from strength, and we get 1% increased energy shield per 2 strength. In my build that is 83% increased energy shield from one node. It isn’t the best option for the slot. A normal rare jewel with a lot of increased energy shield and increased damage gives equivalent energy shield with damage on top, and saves three points. I use this one because it is cheap, and a better rare jewel will probably beat it.

      We are always shapeshifted, so those nodes apply all the time. **Spirit of the Wolf** and **Frozen Claw** are not flexible. **Frozen Claw** gives extra damage as cold and **Spirit of the Wolf** gives critical hit chance and skill speed. **Molten Claw** and **Electrified Claw** do the same for fire and lightning. **Spirit of the Wyvern** gives elemental damage and accuracy, and **Spirit of the Bear** gives increased damage against immobilised enemies, and frozen enemies count as immobilised. **Hide of the Bear**, **Scales of the Wyvern**, and **Blood of the Wolf** each add 1% to a maximum resistance while shapeshifted, which is how my maximum resistances reach 76%. **Scales of the Wyvern** also speeds up energy shield recharge. **Blood of the Wolf** is the flexible one. I don’t use life leech or life regeneration, so I only take it for the maximum cold resistance. Those three points can go into damage if you need it.

      My **Megalomaniac** gives me **Primal Rage**, **Ancient Aegis**, and **Dangerous Blossom**. **Primal Rage** is plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, and I recommend getting it however you can. If you can’t get it on a **Megalomaniac**, anoint it instead of **Zarokh’s Gift**. **Ancient Aegis** boosts the armour and energy shield of our body armour. You don’t need it, but it is a lot of tankiness. If you have spare points and no **Megalomaniac**, you can travel to it.

      We use two weapon sets. The first has the war cry nodes, which only apply to **Arctic Howl**, and the second has the shapeshifted damage nodes and **Potent Incantation**, which does nothing for the war cry. **Guttural Roar** gives war cry speed and area, and it makes war cries debilitate enemies, which slows them and makes them deal less damage, so it also makes us tankier. **Deafening Cries** gives war cry cooldown recovery and 8% increased damage for each time you have war cried recently, and every echo from **Echoing Cry** counts. **Explosive Empowerment** gives empowered attacks increased damage, **Howling Beast** makes war cries inflict critical weakness, and **Extinguishing Exhalation** removes ignite when you war cry. That last one isn’t needed, but it is good to have.
