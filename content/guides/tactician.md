      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Tactician**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Tactician** is a mercenary ascendancy, and it does something no other werewolf ascendancy can: it gives us a pile of free spirit. It also has damage, defence, and mobility, and it is one of the most fun ones to play. The rotation is the one from the general guide, and nothing changes in it.

      The trick starts with the keystone **Lord of the Wilds**. It lets us equip a non-unique sceptre while wielding a talisman. That sounds like a bad deal at first, because it gives 50% less spirit and non-minion skills have 50% less reservation efficiency. The reason to want a sceptre is that a **Shrine Sceptre** grants **Purity of Fire**, and every support gem we socket into that skill costs no spirit. We get five support slots for free, and we fill them with the supports that are expensive anywhere else. Both penalties are paid off. **A Solid Plan** gives persistent buffs 50% less reservation, which cancels the reservation efficiency penalty on **Berserk**, **Herald of Ice**, and our other buffs. The spirit penalty is covered by the increased spirit on the sceptre and its runes. We end up with the spirit we would have had anyway, plus five free supports.

      Defence comes from **Stay Light, Use Cover**, which makes us defend with 200% of our armour and gives enemies an accuracy penalty that grows with distance. The price is that our maximum chance to evade and our maximum physical damage reduction are both 50%. That changes how we build the defence: we stack armour and evasion only up to the caps, and put everything else into the stats the caps do not touch. The Passive Tree section explains how. On top of that we have a large life pool, plenty of life regeneration, and the **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel**, which makes every hit smaller.

      The damage comes from being surrounded, as on every mercenary. Surrounded means five or more enemies in our presence, and the mercenary side of the tree is full of strong bonuses while we are surrounded. That is unreliable, so we wear **Constricting Command**, which makes us need 4 fewer enemies. We are a melee character, so whatever we are hitting is in our presence, and we are surrounded all the time. The rest is critical hits, rage, and freezing enemies quickly, since a frozen enemy takes a lot more damage from us.

  - title: "Skill Gems"
    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Tactician** changes.

      **Purity of Fire**. The **Shrine Sceptre** grants it. The fire resistance aura is nice, but the point is the five support slots that cost no spirit. **Her Declaration** intimidates enemies in our presence, so they take more damage from us and deal less to us. **Seraph’s Heart** gives enemies a chance to calculate their hits as though our resistances were 90%, which is a lot of effective health. **Vitality II** gives life regeneration, which **Berserk** and **Sacrifice of Flesh** both need. **Clarity II** and **Precision II** give mana regeneration and accuracy. None of these five is required, and any support for a persistent buff can take their place. They are free, so fill the slots.

      **Berserk**. **Dialla’s Desire** is the usual one. **Berserk** drains our life, so **Vitality I** gives some regeneration back, **Clarity I** helps with mana, and **Strong Hearted** shortens the shock we receive.

      **Eternal Rage**. **Uhtred’s Omen** adds levels to a skill that has exactly one other support, so **Eternal Rage** runs it with **Armour Demolisher II** as that one support. We have the spirit, so the skill sits in a gem socket and we do not need an **Absent Amulet** to carry it.

      **Shred**. **Rage III** takes the place of **Crescendo II**. It makes our melee hits grant rage, and rage matters here because **Lunar Blessing** lasts longer the more rage we have, and **Berserk** amplifies it.

      **Arctic Howl**. Without **Pack Encouragement** we have no minions to feed **Living Lightning II**, so **Raging Cry** takes its place and makes the warcry grant rage depending on the power of the enemies it hits. **Blazing Critical** takes the place of **Paquate’s Pact**, and it fits a build that crits as much as this one does.

      **Pounce**. It runs **Freezing Mark** as in the general guide, with **Holy Descent** in place of **Culling Strike II**. **Pounce** is a leap, so **Holy Descent** leaves Consecrated Ground where we land.

      **Herald of Ice**. It uses the setup with **Armour Break III**, **Armour Explosion**, and **Uruk’s Smelting**, because the **Megalomaniac** allocates **Dangerous Blossom**.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[tactician-full-endgame]

      The rare items below are described by their mods, because the bases change for everyone depending on their resistances, attributes, and the rest of their gear.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Maji Talisman**, for the maximum rage. I want levels of melee skills, physical damage, flat physical and fire damage, strength, and attack speed. The tree has nodes that boost two-handed weapons, and a talisman counts. I socket **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** for extra elemental damage and a **Perfect Iron Rune** for physical damage. The **Perfect Iron Rune** also increases the effect of fully broken armour, which suits the armour break in this build.

      **Sceptre**. A rare **Shrine Sceptre** that grants **Purity of Fire**. It exists for spirit, so I want increased spirit, increased effect of socketed augment items, intelligence, and maximum mana. Aura magnitude is a bonus. **Rabbit Idol** gives increased spirit on a sceptre, and the **Perfect Robust Rune** and **Perfect Resolve Rune** give strength and intelligence, which keeps our attributes level for **Jack of all Trades**.

      **Helmet**. **Constricting Command**. It makes us need 4 fewer enemies to be surrounded. Normally you need 5 enemies in your presence. With this helmet you need 1, and because we are a melee character, whatever we are hitting is in our presence, so we are surrounded all the time. That unlocks the surrounded bonuses I described in the introduction. It has a **Raven-Touched Shard** in the socket, which lets me anoint it with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket, and **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** for plus 4 maximum rage.

      **Amulet**. A normal rare amulet. An **Absent Amulet** carries **Eternal Rage**, but it gives up two modifiers to do it, and a normal amulet keeps all six. We have the spirit to run **Eternal Rage** ourselves, so the extra modifiers are free damage. I want levels of melee skills, critical hit chance, spirit, maximum life, evasion, and damage taken recouped as life. It is anointed with **Catalysis** for elemental damage with attacks. If you want more skill slots instead, take the **Absent Amulet** and two **Unset Ring**s.

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. Every filled socket gives life, elemental resistances, and spirit, so it carries a lot of what the build needs. **Ancient Aegis** from the **Megalomaniac** also boosts its armour. I run two **Perfect Body Rune**s for life, a **Greater Ward Rune** for the runic ward that **Uhtred’s Chalice** needs, **The Greatwolf’s Rune of Willpower** for 15% of damage taken from mana before life, and **Tecrod’s Gaze** for life regeneration.

      **Belt**. A rare belt whose base has charm slots, because the build runs three charms. I want armour, maximum life, maximum mana, strength, chaos resistance, and life regeneration.

      **Flasks**. A life flask with charge generation and increased recovery, and **Uhtred’s Chalice**. The chalice lets our mana overflow above its maximum, which fixes our mana, and since we take some damage from mana before life, it makes us tankier. It makes us lose 5% of our life per second while we have no runic ward, so the **Greater Ward Rune** in the body armour gives 25 maximum runic ward, which is enough.

      **Rings**. Two rare rings. We want flat damage to attacks, resistances, and a defensive mod. Evasion is the common one, but **Stay Light, Use Cover** caps our chance to evade at 50%, so extra evasion is wasted. Maximum life is the better mod to take on both rings.

      **Gloves**. Rare armour gloves, because **Leather Bound Gauntlets** in the tree gives evasion rating for the armour on them. I want armour, flat damage to attacks, levels of melee skills, strength, and the mod that makes some of our armour also apply to elemental damage. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and mine holds a rare **Emerald** with evasion, critical hit chance, attack damage, and critical damage bonus.

      **Boots**. Rare armour and evasion boots. I want 30% movement speed, maximum life, strength, the mod that makes some of our armour also apply to elemental damage, and the mod that gives deflection rating equal to a share of our evasion rating. A **Soul Core of Tacati** adds chaos resistance.

      **Charms**. A **Thawing Charm** so you never stay frozen, **Beira’s Anguish**, which makes us immune to ignite when we are ignited, and **Forsaken Bangle**, which triggers when we take chaos damage from a hit and recoups half of the chaos damage it prevents as life and mana.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      On the ascendancy I take **A Solid Plan**, **Polish That Gear**, **Stay Light, Use Cover**, and **Suppressing Fire**, plus the spirit and the two armour and evasion nodes on the way.

      **Stay Light, Use Cover** is the node to understand, because used badly it makes us less tanky than a build without it. Our maximum chance to evade and our maximum physical damage reduction are both 50%, so everything above 50% in either one does nothing for us. We take enough armour and evasion to reach the caps, and no more. The surrounded nodes raise both in combat, so a sheet a little under the caps outside of combat is fine, because we reach them while we fight. After that we put our points into the stats the caps do not touch. **Polish That Gear** gives deflection rating equal to 20% of our armour on top of what evasion already gives, and it also turns our evasion rating into ailment threshold. The share of our armour that also applies to elemental damage is not limited by the cap either, so we take **Tempered Defences** and look for it on gear.

      **Suppressing Fire** gives 40% more immobilisation buildup. Freezing counts as immobilisation, and we spend our fights freezing enemies with **Lunar Assault**, so it makes the whole rotation faster. A frozen enemy takes extra damage from **Thin Ice**, **Heavy Frost** ignores its resistances, and **Shred** and **Cross Slash** both want a frozen target.

      Being surrounded is the other half of the build. **Thrill of Battle** gives 20% increased attack speed while surrounded, **In the Thick of It** regenerates 2.5% of our maximum life per second, **Favourable Odds** gives block chance, deflection, and ailment protection, **Last Stand** and **Frantic Fighter** give attack damage, and **Tactical Retreat** gives movement speed. The small nodes around them give attack damage, armour, and evasion while surrounded. **Frantic Fighter** costs us accuracy, and **Precision II** on the sceptre gives some back.

      **Jack of all Trades** gives 2% increased damage for every 5 points of our lowest attribute, so it rewards balanced attributes. The attribute nodes on the tree can be set to strength, dexterity, or intelligence, and I set most of them to dexterity or intelligence, since strength comes easily from gear and runes. Then I raise the lowest attribute on gear until the three are close. When they are, the node is a lot of damage.

      The rest of the damage is critical hits and rage. **Overwhelming Strike**, **Tainted Strike**, and **Spirit of the Wolf** give critical hit chance and more, and the small nodes add critical hit chance and critical damage bonus. **Crushing Verdict**, **Overwhelm**, and **Singular Purpose** give attack damage and two-handed damage and cost 5% attack speed each, and **Sand in the Eyes** and the surrounded attack speed pay that back. For rage I take **Battle Trance** and the small maximum rage nodes.

      The **Time-Lost Emerald** sits where several of the notables we take are in range. It makes notables also grant critical hit chance and critical damage bonus, and small passives also grant evasion rating and elemental damage. The other **Emerald**s carry critical hit chance, critical damage bonus, attack damage, and either evasion rating or elemental damage.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage**, **Ancient Aegis**, and **Dangerous Blossom**. **Primal Rage** gives plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, **Ancient Aegis** gives 60% increased armour from our body armour, and **Dangerous Blossom** gives 10% of damage as extra physical damage. **From Nothing** lets us allocate the nodes in the radius of **Chaos Inoculation** without a connected path, and three of them are what we want: **Heavy Frost**, which is on every werewolf build, **Pure Chaos**, which is 11% of damage as extra chaos damage, and **Thin Ice**.

      The last piece is the **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel**. **Zealot’s Oath** is the only keystone in its range, and the jewel turns it into **Sacrifice of Flesh**. When we take damage from hits, 20% of the life loss is prevented, and then we lose 150% of what was prevented over 4 seconds. So a hit lands as 80% of its damage at once, and the rest arrives slowly. That cuts the burst that kills a character, and we repay it with plenty of life regeneration: **Constricting Command**, **Tecrod’s Gaze**, **Vitality I** and **Vitality II**, the belt, the flask, **In the Thick of It**, and the life recouped from damage on the amulet. It is a big part of why the build is so tanky, and I recommend it. If you have the damage to spare, I still prefer to give up a little speed for tankiness, because not dying makes you faster in the end.
