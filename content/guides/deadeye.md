      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Deadeye**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Deadeye** is a ranger ascendancy, and it is the fastest werewolf. It is probably not the strongest, and I would send you to the **Shaman**, **Oracle**, **Ritualist**, or **Spirit Walker** if raw power is what you want. I play it because it is unusual, and because it works much better than I expected. With weak gear it is rough, so I recommend leveling as something else and swapping to the werewolf once you have a good talisman. The rotation is the one from the general guide, and nothing changes in it.

      Two ascendancy notables are the reason to play it. **Called Shots** is a skill that automatically marks nearby enemies with a Mark gem socketed into it. We socket **Sniper’s Mark**, so we are constantly marking, and every critical hit against a marked enemy deals extra damage and gives us a Frenzy Charge. **Gathering Winds** gives us **Tailwind**, a stacking buff that grants movement speed, skill speed, and evasion rating. A melee character uses skills so fast that the stacks fill almost instantly, and because we stack evasion, we are rarely hit and rarely lose them. Between the two, the **Deadeye** gets more speed than any other werewolf.

      For defence we lean on evasion and deflection. **Grace of the Ancestors** makes every point of rage increase our evasion rating, **Berserk** makes our rage stronger, and the life regeneration we take to pay for **Berserk** also keeps us alive. Evasion builds have one weakness: deflection is capped at 95%, so every so often a hit gets through at full size. The **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel** covers that by turning part of every hit into damage over time.

      For damage we go all in on critical hits. The **Spiny Talisman** has a high base critical hit chance, the jewels and the tree add critical hit chance and critical damage bonus, and **Charge Regulation** gives us more critical hit chance while we have a Power Charge and more skill speed while we have a Frenzy Charge. The flat attack damage on the gloves and rings, plus extra damage from the talisman’s runes, supplies the rest.

    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Deadeye** changes.

      **Called Shots**. It applies the Mark gem socketed into it to a nearby unmarked enemy every few seconds, and it reapplies the mark to another enemy whenever the mark is consumed. **Sniper’s Mark** goes in it, and it should not be **Freezing Mark**. When we activate **Freezing Mark** we get a buff, and marking another target while we have the buff removes it. **Called Shots** keeps marking, so we would lose the buff all the time. **Sniper’s Mark** gives extra critical damage bonus and a Frenzy Charge each time it is consumed. The supports on it are **Charged Mark** for shocked ground, **Mark of Siphoning II** for mana leech, **Charge Profusion II** for extra Frenzy Charges, and **Cooldown Recovery II**, because **Sniper’s Mark** has a cooldown.

      **Pounce**. **Charged Mark** moved to **Sniper’s Mark**, so **Holy Descent** takes its place and leaves Consecrated Ground where we land. It runs **Mark for Death** and **Brutus’ Brain**, because this build has **Pack Encouragement**. The wolves it summons give us damage through **Pack Encouragement** and **Predator’s Mark**. **Mark for Death** breaks armour when the enemy takes physical damage, and this build deals a lot of it.

      **Cross Slash**. **Heft** and **Ailith’s Chimes** replace **Elemental Armament II** and **Execute III**. **Culmination II** makes **Cross Slash** gain combo from our other melee attacks and spend it all on the slash. **Ailith’s Chimes** turns the combo we spend into Power Charges, and **Heft** raises the maximum physical damage of the hit.

      **Charge Regulation**. It needs no support. While active it gives increased skill speed with a Frenzy Charge and more critical hit chance with a Power Charge, but it consumes one of each charge every 10 seconds. That does not matter, because **Sniper’s Mark** and **Ailith’s Chimes** keep both charges up almost all the time. It is one of the gems you do not drop.

      **Eternal Rage**. The **Absent Amulet** grants it, so it costs no spirit. **Uhtred’s Omen** adds levels to a skill with exactly one other support, and **Vitality I** is that support.

      **Berserk**. **Dialla’s Desire** is the usual one. **Berserk** drains life while we are not losing rage, so **Vitality II** gives the regeneration to pay for it.

      **Wind Dancer**. This is the defensive layer I use on evasion characters. **Blind II** and **Maim** make the hit it deals when we are hit apply their effects, and **Magnified Area II** widens it.

      **Mana Remnants**. Werewolves are hungry for mana, and this fixes it. It works from critical hits against enemies with an elemental ailment, and we are critting frozen enemies all the time. **Harmonic Remnants II** and **Remnant Potency I** support it. It needs a skill slot and spirit, and that is the reason for the **Unset Ring** below.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[deadeye-full-endgame]

      The rare items below are described by their mods, because the bases change for everyone depending on their resistances, attributes, and the rest of their gear. The exceptions are the ones where the base is the point.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Spiny Talisman**. It has a high base critical hit chance, and this build wants critical hits. I want levels of melee skills, physical damage, flat physical and fire damage, critical hit chance, and life gained per kill. **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** gives extra elemental damage, and **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony** gives extra chaos damage. A good talisman is where the damage comes from, so it is the first item to upgrade.

      **Helmet**. A rare helmet with evasion. I want increased evasion, critical hit chance, maximum life, a resistance, and the mod that gives deflection rating equal to a share of our evasion rating. It has a **Raven-Touched Shard** in its socket, which lets me anoint it with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet**. It grants **Eternal Rage** at no spirit cost, which leaves the spirit for **Berserk**, **Wind Dancer**, and **Mana Remnants**. The price is one prefix and one suffix. It is anointed with **Grace of the Ancestors**, which gives 10% increased attack speed and 1% increased evasion rating for every point of rage. I want maximum life, levels of melee skills, spirit, and resistances on it. Before you have it, **Eternal Rage** costs 100 spirit, and you have to give something up. **Mana Remnants** is the first thing I would drop, and **Uhtred’s Chalice** can cover the mana for a while. After that, **Berserk** and **Wind Dancer** can go. **Charge Regulation**, **Herald of Ice**, and **Eternal Rage** are the three you do not drop.

      **Rings**. Two rare rings, and one of them should be an **Unset Ring**, for the extra skill slot. Between the two I want flat physical, fire, cold, and lightning damage to attacks, resistances, maximum life, and attributes. **Ingenuity** on the belt increases the bonuses from both rings, so the rings matter more than usual.

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. Every filled socket gives life, elemental resistances, and spirit, so it carries a lot of what the build needs. I fill it with three **Soul Core of Tacati** for chaos resistance and **Tecrod’s Gaze** for life regeneration, which helps pay for **Berserk**.

      **Belt**. **Ingenuity**. It increases the bonuses from both of our rings.

      **Gloves**. Rare evasion gloves. I want increased evasion, flat lightning and physical damage to attacks, levels of melee skills, critical damage bonus, and deflection rating equal to a share of our evasion. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and mine holds a rare **Emerald** with evasion, attack damage, critical hit chance, and critical damage bonus. Before you have enough damage, wear **Sine Aequo** instead. It immobilises enemies at 50% buildup, and freezing counts as immobilising, so enemies freeze faster. Freeze buildup depends on the damage we deal, so a freeze is hard to land when damage is low. **Sine Aequo** also adds damage against immobilised enemies. Switch to the rare gloves once you have the damage for them.

      **Boots**. Rare evasion boots. I want the highest movement speed, maximum life, evasion, a lightning resistance, and spirit. I socket a **Greater Iron Rune** for increased armour, evasion, and energy shield.

      **Charms**. A **Thawing Charm** so you never stay frozen, **Beira’s Anguish**, which makes us immune to ignite when we are ignited, and **Forsaken Bangle**, which triggers when we take chaos damage from a hit and recoups half of the chaos damage it prevents as life and mana.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      ::tree-image[deadeye]

      The ascendancy comes first. **Called Shots** and **Gathering Winds** are the two I always take, as I explained in the introduction. After them I take **Bullseye**, which applies 10 Critical Weakness to an enemy whenever we consume a mark on it. We consume marks all the time: **Called Shots** reapplies and consumes **Sniper’s Mark** constantly, and **Pounce** marks as well, so we use two marks. The last two points go into **Projectile Proximity Specialisation**, and there I choose **Point Blank**. It makes projectiles deal 20% more hit damage to targets in the first stretch of their movement. We are not firing projectiles, but the ice fragments that **Shred** drops on a frozen target are projectiles, and they explode on an enemy we are standing next to, so they get the bonus. It matters for single target bosses and does nothing when mapping, but it is still the best thing I found for those last two points. The small ascendancy nodes on the way give mark effect, projectile speed, and skill speed.

      The alternative for those points is **Thrilling Chase**, with **The Frenzied Bear** on the tree. We make a lot of Frenzy Charges, so doubling the benefit from consuming them looks attractive. The problem is that the only thing consuming our charges is **Charge Regulation**, once every 10 seconds, and the word recently in **The Frenzied Bear** means the past 4 seconds. So the bonus is active about 40% of the time, and **Thrilling Chase** only triggers half of those times, which leaves about 20%. I want something consistent, so I pick **Bullseye** and **Point Blank**.

      Next is defence. **Enhanced Reflexes**, **Enduring Deflection**, **Trained Deflection**, **Beastial Skin**, and **Escape Velocity** give evasion rating and deflection, and **Trained Deflection** and **Enduring Deflection** prevent a share of the damage from deflected hits. **Escape Strategy** and **Careful Consideration** are two sides of the same idea. One gives a large bonus to evasion rating if we have been hit recently and a penalty if we have not, and the other does the reverse. With both, one of them is always paying out and the other is costing less, so we have a net increase to evasion rating whether we have been hit or not. **Tailwind** adds evasion rating and prevents a share of the damage from deflected hits for each stack.

      The **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel** is the safety net for the hits that deflection does not stop. **Chaos Inoculation** is the only keystone in its range, and the jewel turns it into **Sacrifice of Flesh**. When we take damage from hits, 20% of the life loss is prevented, and then we lose 150% of what was prevented over 4 seconds. So a hit lands as 80% of its damage at once, and the rest arrives slowly, which softens the hit that would otherwise kill us. We repay the debt with life regeneration from **Tecrod’s Gaze** and the **Vitality** supports, and with our flasks. I recommend the jewel.

      The critical hit side of the tree is the rest of the damage. **Struck Through** gives plus 1% to critical hit chance for attacks, **Serrated Edges**, **Heartstopping**, and **Careful Assassin** give critical hit chance, and **Throatseeker**, **Cooked**, and **Heartbreaking** give critical damage bonus. **Throatseeker** reduces our critical hit chance, **Cooked** reduces our armour, evasion, and energy shield, and **Careful Assassin** reduces our critical damage bonus, and the large bonuses they give pay for that. The **Time-Lost Emerald** makes notables also grant critical hit chance and critical damage bonus, and small passives also grant evasion rating and attack damage. **Heart of the Well** gives extra fire and chaos damage, critical hit chance, critical damage bonus, and life regeneration, and the other **Emerald**s carry evasion, attack damage, critical hit chance, and critical damage bonus.

      The rest is a few damage notables. **Maiming Strike** gives attack damage and a chance to maim, **Stupefy** and **Dizzying Hits** daze and punish dazed enemies, **Material Solidification** adds physical damage as extra and strengthens fully broken armour, **Thin Ice** and **Heavy Frost** reward the frozen enemies we make, **Catalysis** gives elemental damage with attacks, and **Coming Calamity** gives cold damage while **Herald of Ice** is active. **Honed Instincts** gives attack speed.

      **From Nothing** lets us allocate the nodes in the radius of **Pain Attunement** without a connected path. Four notables are in range. **Desensitisation** gives critical damage bonus and reduces the critical damage bonus of hits against us, **Hide of the Bear** gives armour while shapeshifted, **Bringer of Order** gives increased damage, and **Pack Encouragement** gives attack damage for each minion in our presence, which the wolves from **Pounce** provide. **Brutus’ Brain** makes them unable to take damage, so they cannot die.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage** and **The Noble Wolf**. **Primal Rage** gives plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, which means more evasion from **Grace of the Ancestors** and more damage from **Berserk**. **The Noble Wolf** gives increased ailment magnitude and critical hit chance against marked enemies, and **Called Shots** keeps nearly everything near us marked. The jewel’s third enchant is a spell notable that does nothing for a werewolf.
