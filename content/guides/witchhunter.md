      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Witchhunter**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Witchhunter** is a mercenary ascendancy and one of the tankiest in the game. It checks every box I look for: it is comfortable, it deals damage, it is tanky, and it is cheap. The rotation is the one from the general guide, and nothing changes in it.

      Defence comes first, as on every werewolf. The **Witchhunter** has one of the best defensive nodes in the game, **Obsessive Rituals**. It gives up 50% of our armour and evasion rating and grants **Sorcery Ward**, a skill that surrounds us with a barrier. The barrier can take elemental damage from hits equal to 30% of our armour and evasion rating, and it recharges in full a few seconds after it stops taking damage. On its own it only protects against elemental damage, but **Ceremonial Ablution** lets it take physical and chaos damage from hits as well. That is everything except damage over time and ground effects, so it covers almost all of what kills us. Those two get through, so watch out for burning and bleeding ground, and use the charms that make you immune to them.

      Since the barrier is based on armour and evasion, we stack both. We prefer evasion over armour for the barrier, and the reason is that the barrier takes seconds to recharge once it is broken. A hit we evade never touches the barrier, while armour only reduces what the barrier has to absorb. Evasion also feeds deflection, which is mitigation. The tree and gear let one item feed both stats: armour gloves also give evasion, and evasion also gives armour. **Grace of the Ancestors** turns our rage into evasion as well.

      The damage side comes from being surrounded. The mercenary side of the tree is full of nodes that give attack speed, life regeneration, deflection, attack damage, movement speed, armour, and evasion while we are surrounded, and the helmet makes that state easy to reach. The **Witchhunter** adds **Witchbane** and **No Mercy**, which make us deal more damage the more we have beaten an enemy down, and the cooldown recovery on its small nodes helps **Pounce**, **Arctic Howl**, and **Lunar Blessing**.

  - title: "Skill Gems"
    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Witchhunter** changes.

      **Sorcery Ward**. This comes from **Obsessive Rituals**. The barrier is the core of the defence, and it gets stronger with the level of the skill. I socket the gem to raise its level, with **Cold Mastery**, which adds another level to a cold skill. You do not have to cast it. It is a persistent buff, and it works on its own.

      **Shred**. **Rage III** takes the place of **Crescendo II**. It makes our melee hits grant rage. Rage matters more here than elsewhere, because **Grace of the Ancestors** turns every rage into evasion, and evasion is what the barrier is built on.

      **Arctic Howl**. **Raging Cry** takes the place of the lineage support **Paquate’s Pact**. It makes the warcry grant rage depending on the power of the enemies it affects, which feeds the same evasion.

      **Berserk**. **Dialla’s Desire** and **Uhtred’s Augury** are the usual ones, and **Uhtred’s Augury** needs exactly two other supports. **Vitality II** is the third, and it gives life regeneration while the skill is active.

      **Eternal Rage**. The **Absent Amulet** carries it, so it costs no spirit. **Vitality I** is the support it has in the Full build.

      **Savage Fury**. It sits in the second weapon set, as in the general guide, so it costs no spirit and still charges while we fight. Keep it up and we have onslaught and extra damage all the time. **Prolonged Duration II** is its only support.

      **Wind Dancer**. This is the evasion defensive layer from the general guide. **Life Leech III** keeps our life up, and **Maim**, **Blind II**, and **Magnified Area II** apply to the hit it releases around us when we are struck.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[witchhunter-full-endgame]

      The rare items below are described by their mods, because the bases change for everyone depending on their resistances, attributes, and the rest of their gear. Besides the jewellery, everything we wear is evasion or armour.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Maji Talisman**, for the maximum rage. I want levels of melee skills, physical damage, fire damage, strength, and attack speed. The tree has nodes that boost two-handed weapons, and a talisman counts. I socket **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** and a **Perfect Iron Rune**. This build does not go critical, so the talisman does not need critical hit chance.

      **Helmet**. **Constricting Command**. It makes us need 4 fewer enemies to be surrounded. Normally you need 5 enemies in your presence. With this helmet you need 1, and because we are a melee character, whatever we are hitting is in our presence, so we are surrounded all the time. That unlocks the surrounded bonuses I listed in the introduction. It has a **Raven-Touched Shard** in the socket, which lets me anoint it with **Grace of the Ancestors**. It also has the **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** for plus 4 maximum rage. If you do not have the shard, do not anoint it, and take the node some other way.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**. I want evasion, levels of melee skills, resistances, and spirit. **Zarokh’s Gift** is anointed on it for an extra jewel socket.

      **Body armour**. **Hyrri’s Ire**. It gives a lot of evasion, which feeds the barrier, and its evasion is doubled if we have not been hit recently. Since we evade a lot, that is nearly always true. It also gives 20% of damage as extra cold damage, so it is good for both defence and damage. I socket the **Soul Core of Tacati** for chaos resistance, **The Greatwolf’s Rune of Willpower** for 15% of damage taken from mana before life, and **Tecrod’s Gaze** for life regeneration.

      **Belt**. **Ingenuity**. It increases the bonuses we get from the left and right rings. All my rings have at least two sources of flat damage to attacks, and also evasion, and **Ingenuity** amplifies both, so we get more damage and more barrier. The price is fewer charm charges.

      **Flasks**. A life flask, and **Uhtred’s Chalice**. The chalice lets our mana overflow above its maximum, which fixes our mana, and since we take some damage from mana before life, it makes us tankier. It makes us lose 5% of our life per second while we have no runic ward, so the **Greater Ward Rune** in the boots gives 25 maximum runic ward, which is enough. Rune forging the pieces would take evasion away from them.

      **Rings**. Two rare rings. We want flat damage to attacks, evasion, and resistances. If you feel tanky enough and want more damage, take the evasion off and put another flat damage mod on, and **Ingenuity** amplifies it even more.

      **Gloves**. Rare armour gloves, because **Leather Bound Gauntlets** in the tree gives evasion rating for the armour on them, so the gloves count twice for the barrier. I want armour, flat damage to attacks, levels of melee skills, strength, and the mod that makes some of our armour also apply to elemental damage. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and mine holds a rare ruby with life regeneration rate, maximum rage, attack damage, and strength.

      **Boots**. Rare evasion boots. I want 30% movement speed, resistances, and evasion. The **Greater Ward Rune** is in the socket.

      **Charms**. A **Thawing Charm** so you never stay frozen, **Beira’s Anguish**, which makes us immune to ignite when we are ignited, and **Forsaken Bangle**, which triggers when we take chaos damage from a hit and recoups half of the chaos damage it prevents as life and mana.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      On the ascendancy I take **Witchbane**, **No Mercy**, **Obsessive Rituals**, and **Ceremonial Ablution**, plus the two cooldown recovery nodes and the two armour and evasion nodes on the way. **Witchbane** gives enemies a concentration bar equal to 30% of their maximum life, and our hits break it by the damage they deal, and **No Mercy** gives up to 40% more damage against enemies based on how much concentration they are missing. We break a lot of concentration without noticing, so we deal extra damage all the time. It is not only for bosses. It also works on the big rares in your maps, the ones that take several hits to kill, so it is useful everywhere. The cooldown recovery on the small nodes helps **Pounce**, **Arctic Howl**, and **Lunar Blessing**.

      Being surrounded is the other half. **Thrill of Battle** gives 20% increased attack speed while surrounded, **In the Thick of It** regenerates 2.5% of our maximum life per second, **Favourable Odds** gives deflection and ailment protection, **Last Stand** and **Frantic Fighter** give attack damage, and **Tactical Retreat** gives movement speed. The small nodes around them give attack damage, armour, and evasion while surrounded. **Frantic Fighter** costs us accuracy, and we do not care.

      The defence is also in the tree. Besides the barrier, I take every armour and evasion node I can reach. **Leather Bound Gauntlets** gives evasion rating for the armour on our gloves, **General’s Bindings** gains a share of our evasion as armour, **Tempered Defences** makes part of our armour apply to elemental damage, and **Defiance**, **Feel no Pain**, **Backup Plan**, and **Battle-hardened** add more. **Afterimage** gives evasion after we hit, **Hide of the Bear** gives armour and stun threshold while shapeshifted, and **Presence Present** gives damage while an ally is in our presence. The more evasion we stack, the more barrier we have. The barrier is higher when we are out fighting than in the hideout, because every one of those nodes also works when we are surrounded.

      For damage I take **Crushing Verdict**, **Overwhelm**, **Singular Purpose**, **Unexpected Finesse**, and the two-handed damage nodes. I socket **From Nothing** on **Pain Attunement**, so I can allocate the nodes in its radius without a connected path. That gets me **Pack Encouragement**, **Bringer of Order**, and **Desensitisation**, without the nodes around them that I do not want. The rest is maximum rage: **Battle Trance**, **Primal Rage**, and the small maximum rage nodes. The **Time-Lost Ruby** goes where many of the notables are allocated, and it makes notables grant life regeneration rate, stun buildup, and plus 1 maximum rage, and small passives grant melee damage. The other rare rubies carry life regeneration rate, melee damage, attack damage, and plus 2 maximum rage. This build can be tanky as a choice, and it can give up some of that tankiness for damage. Since the barrier protects our life, the build is a good fit for a low life setup if you want to take it further.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Heavy Frost** and **Pure Chaos**. **Heavy Frost** is on every werewolf build, and **Pure Chaos** is a lot of damage. The helmet has **Grace of the Ancestors**, which is hidden from the tree and can only be reached by an anoint or a **Megalomaniac**. It gives 10% increased attack speed, and every rage also grants 1% increased evasion rating. We stack rage, **Berserk** amplifies it, rage becomes evasion, evasion becomes deflection and barrier, and everything compounds.
