      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Chronomancer**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Chronomancer** is a sorceress ascendancy, so we start on the spell side of the tree with a lot of spell damage and energy shield. The **Crown of Eyes** makes increases to spell damage apply to attacks as well, which turns all of it into werewolf damage. Rage works twice for us, because **Mystical Rage** makes every rage also grant increased spell damage, and **Berserk** amplifies both. Compared to the **Stormweaver** you give up a little damage, but the build is much more comfortable to play.

      The ascendancy adds defence and speed. **Phased Form** makes us take 30% less damage, and 4 seconds after we are hit we take 30% of that hit’s damage. **Quicksand Hourglass** grants **Sands of Time**, which gives skill speed and area of effect that scale in opposite directions, so on average we get about 30% of each.

      The defence is regeneration. **Reverie** makes our non-unique life flasks apply their effects constantly, and **Shavronne’s Satchel** makes that recovery apply to energy shield as well. In my case that is 67.6 life regeneration plus about 900 recovery per second from the flask, so roughly 1,000 per second, all the time. I don’t take **Chaos Inoculation**, and the Passive Tree section explains why.

      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups), and **Shred**, **Pounce**, **Lunar Assault**, and **Cross Slash** use their standard setups unchanged. Everything below is what the **Chronomancer** changes.

      **Temporal Rift**. This comes from **Footprints in the Sand**. It constantly leaves afterimages of where you were, and casting it takes you back to the oldest one, restoring your life, mana, and energy shield to what they were then and removing any damage still waiting from **Phased Form**. You stay a werewolf when you use it. It is the emergency button: if something goes wrong and your life starts dropping, press it. I rarely reach the point of needing it. **Cooldown Recovery II** is the support that matters here, and the other slots are not important. **Rite of Restoration** comes from **Reverie** and we ignore it.

      **Eternal Rage**. **Reverie** gives no spirit, so we use an **Absent Amulet** to supply **Eternal Rage**. **Uhtred’s Omen** raises its level, and it works because **Herbalism I** is the one other support. **Herbalism I** increases life recovery from flasks while the skill is active. **Reverie** applies our flask every second, so we get healed a lot more.

      **Overwhelming Presence**. I use rare gloves instead of **Sine Aequo** in this build because it needed the damage, so we lose freezing at 50% buildup. **Overwhelming Presence** lowers the elemental ailment threshold of enemies in our presence, and they freeze much faster. **Herbalism II** goes on it, and it stacks with the **Herbalism I** on **Eternal Rage** because they are on different skills. Together they almost double the regeneration the flask gives us.

      **Arctic Howl**. **Living Lightning II** triggers on each of the three yells from **Echoing Cry** and summons small lightnings. They deal a little damage and count as minions in our presence, so they raise **Predator’s Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**. It only triggers when the skill deals lightning damage, which **Electrified Claw** in the tree provides. Without that node, use **Cold Mastery** instead. **Vruun’s Aftermath** empowers three additional attacks when the war cry destroys at least five ice crystals or ice fragments. **Shred** drops ice fragments on a frozen target, so it happens by itself sometimes, and you don’t need to aim for it. The number shown over the skill is your empowered attacks, usually two to five, and over ten when things go well.

      **Lunar Blessing**. We use **Blood Magic**, so the skill costs life, and **Efficiency II** keeps it safe. It isn’t essential, and if you are willing to take more risk, **Cold Mastery** works in its place. **Vorana’s Siege** is only for damage and area, so without it you can use **Cold Mastery** or **Magnified Area II**.

      **Herald of Ice**. **Dangerous Blossom** gives 10% of damage as extra physical damage, which lets me run the armour break setup from the general guide, where **Herald of Ice** and **Armour Explosion** keep triggering each other and clear bigger screens. If you can’t get **Dangerous Blossom**, skip the armour break and use the standard setup with **Elemental Focus**, **Magnified Area II**, and **Cold Mastery**.

      **Berserk**. This one is essential, because it amplifies both the attack damage and the spell damage from rage. **Dialla’s Desire** and **Uhtred’s Augury** improve it further and are not needed. **Vitality I** gives life regeneration, which **Zealot’s Oath** applies to energy shield, but it costs 20 spirit and I only fit it because my amulet has 50 spirit. You can drop it.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[chronomancer-full-endgame]

      **Weapon**. A **Maji Talisman**, for the maximum rage. Physical damage matters more than elemental damage, so get as much as you can, then elemental damage. After that comes another attribute, either intelligence or strength, then attack speed, then levels of attack skills. Mine is far from perfect and still clears everything.

      **Helmet**. The **Crown of Eyes**, which turns all the spell damage on this side of the tree into attack damage. I have the **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** for plus 4 maximum rage. **Dangerous Blossom** is anointed on it, which I can do because the helmet has a **Raven-Touched Shard**. If you don’t have that, don’t anoint it and don’t run the armour break. You can also get **Dangerous Blossom** from a **Megalomaniac**.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**, because **Reverie** gives no spirit. It doesn’t need to be a perfect 50 spirit, but it needs enough for **Herald of Ice**, **Berserk**, **Overwhelming Presence**, **Herbalism I**, and **Herbalism II**. That is at least 30 spirit if you drop **Vitality I**. After spirit I want energy shield, either flat or increased, levels of melee skills, and all elemental resistances. The last one matters because the **Crown of Eyes** and **Reverie** each take 10% fire resistance, so balancing resistances is hard. It doesn’t need to be perfect. The one I used before had all elemental resistances, spirit, and melee skills, plus a useless mana roll, and it did the same job. Mine is anointed with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket.

      **Body armour**. **Reverie** is what makes everything possible. Non-unique life flasks apply their effects constantly, so we never press them. The recovery can’t be instant and can’t be applied to anyone but us. It also reduces life flask recovery. For the sockets I use **Perfect Vision Rune** in all three, for increased life and mana recovery from flasks. You can put resistances or attributes there if you are missing them, but recovery is the whole reason to wear **Reverie**. A **Reverie** with more sockets and a good roll is an upgrade.

      **Belt**. **Shavronne’s Satchel**, which makes life flask recovery also apply to energy shield. Without it **Reverie** is useless for us, because the flask would only heal a life pool we don’t rely on. It also reduces flask life recovery rate, so we want a roll close to the best. Mine is 21%, and a good roll is not expensive.

      **Flask**. An **Ultimate Life Flask**, the one with the most regeneration, rolled with as much increased life recovered as possible. It also has a modifier that removes some of the life recovered from mana. We never press the flask, so that downside never applies to us, and it wouldn’t take our mana even if we didn’t use **Blood Magic**. We keep the extra recovery and lose nothing.

      **Rings**. Both are rare **Prismatic Ring** bases, whose implicit fixes resistances. Flat damage to attacks, resistances, and life regeneration, which is good with **Zealot’s Oath** but not mandatory. You may fix your resistances in a better way, but I needed the **Prismatic Ring** implicit and a lot of resistances.

      **Gloves**. Rare gloves with full energy shield, instead of **Sine Aequo**. We want flat damage, levels of skills, resistances, and an attribute. Mine has dexterity, and it could just as well be strength or intelligence, because you fix the other attributes in the tree. **Overwhelming Presence** and the freeze buildup in the tree replace what **Sine Aequo** would have done.

      **Boots**. **Sekhema Sandals** with full energy shield, like the gloves. We are a full energy shield build and not a hybrid with armour, and the mitigation comes from **Phased Form**. I have fire resistance, chaos resistance, life regeneration, and movement speed on mine. The **Legacy of Wanderlust** rune makes our speed unaffected by slows. I don’t care about slows to movement speed, but I do care about slows to attack speed, and this protects it. It is very expensive and not necessary, so only get it if you can afford it.

      **Charms**. A **Thawing Charm** so you never get frozen. I have **Nascent Hope**, but a rare one is better for me, because I don’t use energy shield recharge. I use **Forsaken Bangle** to cap chaos resistance. I sit at 61%, and when it activates I reach the cap. If you are close to the cap and not at it, an **Amethyst Charm** gets you there. The third is **Rite of Passage**, for the Spirit of the Owl, which gives energy shield and a good amount of damage and is cheap. **For Utopia** is the alternative if you want stun immunity and armour.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      On the ascendancy I take **Quicksand Hourglass**, **Apex of the Moment**, **Phased Form**, **Footprints in the Sand**, and both **Buff Expiry Rate** nodes. **Sands of Time** gives 60% increased skill speed that scales down to 1% over 10 seconds, while 1% increased area of effect scales up to 60%, and then they swap back. On average we get about 30% of each, and both are what the werewolf wants. **Apex of the Moment** slows enemies in our presence by 20%. Against something that can only attack you, that is 20% fewer attacks, which is tankiness. **Buff Expiry Rate** makes our buffs expire 10% slower per node, so **Lunar Blessing** and **Savage Fury** last longer.

      **Phased Form** is the reason the build works the way it does. With energy shield recharge, you get hit, wait for the recharge delay, and start recharging, and then the delayed 30% from **Phased Form** hits you and interrupts it. In melee you take damage constantly, from monsters or from **Phased Form**, so energy shield recharge is never reliable. That is why I take **Zealot’s Oath** instead of **Chaos Inoculation**. It applies excess life recovery from regeneration to energy shield and stops energy shield from recharging, which doesn’t matter to us. We also don’t need a huge pool. A character without mitigation needs 10k energy shield, but we take 30% less damage, so our biggest hit is smaller. The problem of the **Chronomancer** is recovery and not the maximum hit, so we fix the recovery with **Reverie**.

      **Blood Magic** solves mana completely, and the werewolf is very mana hungry. Our skills cost life instead, and with the regeneration and the flask I barely notice the cost. Even **Lunar Blessing**, which uses the most, is back up very quickly. With **Blood Magic** you never think about mana pool or mana regeneration.

      My life regeneration nodes are optional. If you drop them, drop the life regeneration from your gear as well, and spend the points on damage or something else you need. I like having a little extra on top of the flask. **Chakra of Life** gives 3% increased maximum life and 10% increased life recovery rate. Life recovery rate is not the same as life regeneration rate, which only affects natural regeneration. Recovery applies to every form of life recovery, flasks included, so it amplifies what **Reverie** and **Shavronne’s Satchel** give us. The build works without it, but I like it.

      The **Heroic Tragedy** with the line of Vorana converts a keystone in its radius into **Black Scythe Training**. We gain no inherent bonus from strength, and we get 1% increased energy shield per 2 strength, which is around 80% increased energy shield from one node in my build. You don’t have to use it. A rare jewel with increased energy shield, like a sapphire, saves you three points, and a **Heroic Tragedy** is cheap, while a rare jewel that beats it is much more expensive.

      For freeze, which **Sine Aequo** no longer handles, I take **Inescapable Cold** for 40% increased freeze buildup, and **Heavy Frost** and **Thin Ice** as on all my werewolf builds. With **Heavy Frost**, hits ignore the non-negative elemental resistances of frozen enemies, and with **Rakiata’s Flow** on **Lunar Assault** we don’t need penetration. For that reason I don’t care about the cold penetration on **Glaciation**, which I take for the 6% of elemental damage as extra cold damage. If you don’t have **Rakiata’s Flow**, the penetration becomes very good. **Endless Blizzard** gives plus 1 to the level of all cold skills, and **Abasement** gives stun threshold equal to 30% of maximum energy shield, which basically solves stuns. A **Stone Charm** can do the same and frees three points, but I like it this way.

      **All Natural** gives plus 5% to all elemental resistances and 30% increased elemental damage, and I took it to fix my resistances. I also take the nodes that give increased spell damage and increased attack damage together. Because of the **Crown of Eyes**, each of those counts as 16% increased damage, so I take all four.

      I socket **From Nothing** on **Pain Attunement**, which lets me allocate the nodes in its radius without a connected path. That gets me **Mystical Rage**, which is fundamental, and **Pack Encouragement** for 5% increased attack damage per minion in our presence, up to 80%. Seven wolves from **Pounce** with **Brutus’ Brain** get us close to that. It also gets me **Bringer of Order** for increased damage, **Desensitisation** for critical damage bonus and defence, and **Hide of the Bear** for armour and stun threshold while shapeshifted, without the nodes around them that I don’t want.

      We are always shapeshifted, so those nodes apply all the time. I take **Frozen Claw**, **Electrified Claw**, **Molten Claw**, **Spirit of the Wolf**, **Spirit of the Wyvern**, and **Spirit of the Bear**. **Electrified Claw** is the one this build cannot skip, because it is what triggers **Living Lightning II**. The wyvern, wolf, and bear nodes give elemental damage, speed, and damage against immobilised enemies, and frozen enemies count as immobilised.

      My **Megalomaniac** gives me **Primal Rage** and **Chakra of Life**. **Primal Rage** is plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, and I recommend getting it however you can. If you can’t get it on a **Megalomaniac**, anoint it instead of **Zarokh’s Gift**, or travel to it and move the three points from somewhere else.

      The jewel sockets take rubies with life regeneration rate, damage while shapeshifted, plus maximum rage, and attack damage. A sapphire with spell damage and maximum energy shield also works.

      We use two weapon sets. The first has the war cry nodes, which only apply to **Arctic Howl**, and the second has everything else. The war cry nodes are **Guttural Roar**, **Deafening Cries**, **Explosive Empowerment**, **Howling Beast**, and **Extinguishing Exhalation**. They make the war cry empower more attacks, debilitate enemies, and amplify your critical hits.
