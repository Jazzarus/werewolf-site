      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Disciple of Varashta**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Disciple of Varashta** is a sorceress ascendancy built around djinn. All of its offence is baked into the djinn and their commands, and a werewolf doesn’t command anything, because it swipes, shreds, and pounces. So every point we spend in the ascendancy is defensive. That suits the werewolf, which is weak in defence, and it makes this one of the safest werewolves you can build. If you want to play a werewolf in hardcore, I recommend it. The price is that the ascendancy gives us no damage, so the damage has to come from the tree and the gear.

      The defence is energy shield. **Sacred Rituals** adds 60% of our current energy shield to our armour when the game works out physical damage reduction. That is damage reduction only, and our armour stays at zero. Anything that scales armour does nothing for us, including increased armour and the nodes that make armour apply to elemental damage. Elemental damage is what ruins armour based defences, and **Silks of Veneration** fixes it, because current energy shield also grants elemental damage reduction. Now energy shield is our health pool, our physical mitigation, and our elemental mitigation at once, so we stack as much of it as we can. In my build it is close to 10k out of combat and over 10k in combat, and with that much mitigation on top it is very hard to die.

      The damage comes from the **Crown of Eyes**, which makes the spell damage we collect apply to attacks, and from rage. **Mystical Rage** makes every rage grant increased spell damage, and the **Crown of Eyes** turns that into attack damage too. That is still not enough, so the amulet, gloves, and belt are chosen for damage, and the Skill Gems and Gear sections explain what each of those costs us.

  - title: "Skill Gems"
    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Disciple of Varashta** changes.

      **Navira, the Last Mirage**. This comes from **Barya of Navira**. It summons Navira, a water djinn who only acts when we command her. We don’t want her offence. We want her commands. Her normal one, **Navira’s Embrace**, creates a bubble that absorbs part of the damage dealt by enemies outside it. **Bidding III**, **Rapid Casting II**, **Cold Mastery**, and **Prolonged Duration II** make her commands come back sooner and last longer, and they are not essential.

      **Navira’s Well**. This one comes from the ascendancy and not from a gem. Command Navira to bolster your energy shield, and it begins recharging immediately and recharges faster for 6 seconds. The cooldown is 12 seconds. When your energy shield drops low after a hard hit, press it and it comes back. Navira has to be summoned to use it, which is what the gem is for.

      **Kurgal’s Leash**. This is optional. Put it on **Navira, the Last Mirage** and every time you command her with **Navira’s Embrace** you gain **Unholy Might** for 15 seconds, which is 30% of your damage as extra chaos damage. The cooldown of **Navira’s Embrace** is also 15 seconds, so one press per cooldown keeps it up. It works with **Navira’s Embrace** only, not with **Navira’s Well**. You press **Navira’s Embrace** for the damage and ignore the bubble. If you don’t like pressing buttons, leave it out.

      **Shred**. **Medved’s Felling** takes the place of **Crescendo II**. It makes our attacks faster the more rage we have and grants rage on hit. It also makes us lose life based on our rage, but that cost scales with maximum life, and **Chaos Inoculation** leaves us with 1, so it costs us nothing.

      **Eternal Rage**. The **Absent Amulet** carries it, so it costs us no spirit. **Clarity II** is its only other support, which lets **Uhtred’s Omen** raise its level, because **Uhtred’s Omen** needs exactly one other support. **Clarity II** gives mana regeneration while the skill is active.

      **Overwhelming Presence**. I use rare gloves instead of **Sine Aequo** in this build because the gloves are where we get the most damage, so we lose the immobilise at 50% buildup that **Sine Aequo** gives. **Overwhelming Presence** makes enemies in our presence easier to inflict elemental ailments on, so they freeze much faster, and that makes up for it. It costs spirit, which is why **Eternal Rage** sits on the amulet. **Clarity I** and **Precision I** go on it for mana regeneration and accuracy.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[disciple-of-varashta-full-endgame]

      **Weapon**. A **Maji Talisman**, for the maximum rage. Physical damage matters more than elemental damage, so get as much as you can, then elemental damage. After that comes attack speed, then levels of melee skills. I socket **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** and **Perfect Iron Rune**. Every rune we wear also feeds the **Darkness Enthroned**, so fill every socket you have.

      **Helmet**. The **Crown of Eyes**, which turns all the spell damage on this build into attack damage. I have the **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** for plus 4 maximum rage. **Battle Trance** is anointed on it for plus 8 maximum rage, which I can do because the helmet has a **Raven-Touched Shard**. If you don’t have that, don’t anoint it.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**, which frees our spirit for **Overwhelming Presence**. The **Silks of Veneration** already give us spirit, so the amulet doesn’t need any. I want maximum mana, spell damage, levels of melee skills, and fire and chaos resistance. The **Crown of Eyes** takes 10% of our fire resistance, so the fire resistance matters. Mine is anointed with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket.

      **Body armour**. **Silks of Veneration**. It is the item that makes the build work, because current energy shield also grants elemental damage reduction, so one pool covers physical and elemental. It also gives spirit, mana regeneration, and energy shield recharge rate. A rare chest can have a bigger energy shield pool, but it gives up the elemental damage reduction, which is worth more. I use three **Perfect Iron Rune** for more energy shield.

      **Belt**. **Darkness Enthroned**. It increases the effect of socketed augment items, and this version counts what is socketed in it as if it were gloves. Gloves have the offensive rune sockets, which is why we want it. I use **Emergent Possibility**, which gains a percentage of damage as extra damage of a random element for each rune socketed in our equipment, and **The Greatwolf’s Rune of Claws**, which adds physical damage to attacks. The belt doubles both, so **Emergent Possibility** gives 2% per rune instead of 1%. With the runes in my gear it comes to 16% of damage as extra damage of a random element. That is a big multiplier.

      **Flask**. **Uhtred’s Chalice**. Normally you don’t want it, because it makes you lose life while it is active. With **Chaos Inoculation** we have no life to lose, so the downside never applies. It lets mana overflow above our maximum, which multiplies our mana pool, and a bigger mana pool is a bigger health pool for us, since we take damage from mana before life. It is a mana solution and a defence in one flask, and I recommend it over spending spirit on mana.

      **Rings**. Rare rings. We want flat damage to attacks and resistances. Mine are a **Sapphire Ring** and a **Prismatic Ring**, because their implicit resistances help me reach the cap. The bases are not fixed. Pick whichever bases fix the resistances you are missing, so yours will change depending on your gear, tree, and resistance needs.

      **Gloves**. Rare gloves with full energy shield, instead of **Sine Aequo**. We want flat damage to attacks, levels of melee skills, an attribute, and resistances. Mine has dexterity and fire resistance. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and mine holds a rare sapphire with spell damage, maximum energy shield, and skill speed while shapeshifted. **Overwhelming Presence** replaces what **Sine Aequo** would have done for freezing.

      **Boots**. **Sekhema Sandals** with full energy shield, like the gloves. I want resistances, maximum energy shield, energy shield recharge rate, and movement speed, and I have two **Perfect Iron Rune** in the sockets.

      **Charms**. **Nascent Hope**, a **Thawing Charm** so you never stay frozen, which also starts our energy shield recharge when it triggers. **Beira’s Anguish**, a **Dousing Charm**, which makes us immune to ignite when we are ignited. The third is **Rite of Passage**, for the Spirit of the Owl, which gives 60% increased energy shield and a good amount of damage and is cheap. It is what takes us over 10k energy shield in combat.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      ::tree-image[disciple-of-varashta]

      On the ascendancy I take **Sacred Rituals**, **Varashta’s Intuition**, **The Fourth Teaching**, **Barya of Navira**, and **Navira’s Well**, plus the maximum energy shield and maximum mana nodes along the way. **Varashta’s Intuition** makes 100% of elemental damage be taken from mana before life, and that works like extra health, because mana counts as a health pool. **The Fourth Teaching** removes a second from the energy shield recharge delay and gives 40% more recharge rate while on low energy shield. **Barya of Navira** and **Navira’s Well** give us the recovery button. I skipped **Instruments of Power**, which only helps with a staff and we wield a talisman, and **Baryanic Leylines**, which enlarges non-unique time-lost jewels, because the tree has no good place for one.

      I take **Chaos Inoculation**, since energy shield is our whole health pool. It is also what makes **Uhtred’s Chalice** free. The rest of the tree is stacked with maximum energy shield. I take **Pure Energy**, **Melding**, **Patient Barrier**, **Enhanced Barrier**, **Insightfulness**, and **Dampening Shield**, and **Abasement** gives us stun threshold equal to 30% of our maximum energy shield, which basically solves stuns.

      Recovery has two parts. The first is **Navira’s Well**, which is the button. The second is automatic. We freeze things, so we don’t get hit that much, and our energy shield recharges on its own, so I take nodes that make it start sooner. **Convalescence** and **Scales of the Wyvern** give faster start of recharge, along with the energy shield delay nodes. **Patient Barrier** makes the start slower, and these more than pay for it. That way you don’t have to press **Navira’s Well** every time.

      The three rare sapphires in the tree carry spell damage, maximum energy shield, and a few percent of damage taken from mana before life, which stacks with **Varashta’s Intuition**.

      The **Heroic Tragedy** with the line of Vorana converts a keystone in its radius, here **Blackflame Covenant**, into **Black Scythe Training**. We gain no inherent bonus from strength, and we get 1% increased energy shield per 2 strength, which is a lot of energy shield from one node. You don’t have to use it, and a rare jewel with increased energy shield works in its place.

      For freeze, which **Sine Aequo** no longer handles, I take **Inescapable Cold** for 40% increased freeze buildup, and **Heavy Frost** and **Thin Ice** as on all my werewolf builds. With **Heavy Frost**, hits ignore the non-negative elemental resistances of frozen enemies, and with **Rakiata’s Flow** on **Lunar Assault** we don’t need penetration. For that reason I don’t care about the cold penetration on **Glaciation**, which I take for the 6% of elemental damage as extra cold damage. **Endless Blizzard** gives plus 1 to the level of all cold skills, and **All Natural** gives plus 5% to all elemental resistances and 30% increased elemental damage.

      I also take the nodes that give increased spell damage and increased attack damage together. Because of the **Crown of Eyes**, each of those counts as 16% increased damage. **Potent Incantation** and **Raw Power** are pure spell damage, which the **Crown of Eyes** also turns into attack damage.

      I socket **From Nothing** on **Pain Attunement**, which lets me allocate the nodes in its radius without a connected path. That gets me **Mystical Rage**, which is fundamental, and **Pack Encouragement** for 5% increased attack damage per minion in our presence, up to 80%. Seven wolves from **Pounce** with **Brutus’ Brain** get us close to that. It also gets me **Bringer of Order** for increased damage, **Desensitisation** for critical damage bonus and defence, and **Hide of the Bear** for maximum fire resistance and stun threshold while shapeshifted, without the nodes around them that I don’t want. The armour on **Hide of the Bear** does nothing for us.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage**, **Ancient Aegis**, and **Dangerous Blossom**. **Primal Rage** is plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted. **Ancient Aegis** gives 60% increased energy shield from our body armour, which is a lot with the **Silks of Veneration**. **Dangerous Blossom** gives 10% of damage as extra physical damage, which lets us run the armour break setup on **Herald of Ice** from the general guide.

      We are always shapeshifted, so the shapeshifted nodes apply all the time. I take **Frozen Claw**, **Electrified Claw**, **Molten Claw**, **Spirit of the Wolf**, **Spirit of the Wyvern**, and **Spirit of the Bear**. The wyvern, wolf, and bear nodes give elemental damage, speed, and damage against immobilised enemies, and frozen enemies count as immobilised.

      We use two weapon sets. The first has the war cry nodes, which only apply to **Arctic Howl**, and the second has everything else. The war cry nodes are **Guttural Roar**, **Deafening Cries**, **Explosive Empowerment**, **Howling Beast**, and **Extinguishing Exhalation**. They make the war cry empower more attacks, debilitate enemies, and amplify your critical hits.
