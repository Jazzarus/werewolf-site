  - title: Intro
    content: |
      The Shaman is an exceptionally powerful Werewolf ascendancy built around stacking Rage to increase damage, Armour, and Spell Damage. With **Mystical Rage** and **Crown of Eyes**, Rage contributes to both Attack Damage and Spell Damage, with increases and reductions to Spell Damage also applying to attacks. The result is a build that scales several offensive and defensive stats from the same investment.
      
      The defensive setup combines Armour, Energy Shield, and substantial life regeneration. **Zealot's Oath** redirects life regeneration to Energy Shield, while Rage increases Armour and Berserk amplifies the benefits of Rage. The build is fast, durable, and capable of dealing enormous damage without requiring mirror-tier equipment.
      
      A Headhunter can take the build even further, but it is an upgrade rather than a requirement.

  - title: Core Mechanics
    content: |
      ### Rage Scaling
      
      Rage is the foundation of this build. It provides increased Attack Damage, while **Mystical Rage** adds increased Spell Damage for every point of Rage. **Crown of Eyes** allows increases and reductions to Spell Damage to apply to attacks, so both bonuses contribute to the build's attack damage.
      
      Rage also increases Armour through a separate passive interaction. Berserk amplifies the benefits, making a large Rage pool valuable for damage and defense at the same time.
      
      The build aims for approximately 80 Rage, with further investment in maximum Rage improving its scaling. A **Maji Talisman** with a high maximum Rage roll is particularly useful.
      
      ### Armour, Energy Shield, and Life Regeneration
      
      The defensive setup combines Armour and Energy Shield, supported by a large amount of life regeneration. **Zealot's Oath** makes life regeneration apply to Energy Shield, allowing the build to recover its main defensive resource continuously instead of relying entirely on recharge.
      
      Rage contributes additional Armour, and **Insulating Hide** allows 20% of Armour to apply to Elemental Damage while shapeshifted. This gives the build strong physical mitigation alongside meaningful elemental protection.
      
      **Blood Magic is not part of this version's core setup.** Instead, the build uses mana sustain and a source of mana leech to support its skills. Berserk's life drain makes life regeneration valuable, but it does not replace the need to manage mana.
  - title: Skill Gems and Support Gems
    content: |
      The important decisions are the supports that scale with Rage, maintain the wolf pack, improve Freeze-based damage, and maximize Arctic Howl.
      
      ### Shred
      
      Shred is the main attack used against frozen targets. Prioritize attack speed and damage, and use **Long Fuse** to improve the damage of its ice fragments.
      
      The build also uses Shred to take advantage of **Heavy Frost**. Once an enemy is frozen, Heavy Frost allows hits to ignore non-negative elemental resistances, making Shred substantially more effective.
      
      ### Lunar Assault
      
      Lunar Assault benefits from **Bhatair's Vengeance** when you have a large Rage pool. When you freeze with supported skills, Bhatair's Vengeance grants you and allies in your Presence additional Cold Damage based on your Rage. If you have less Rage, use **Ice Bite** instead to gain additional damage from freezing enemies.
      
      **Elemental Armament** increases damage, while **Ancestral Call** improves clear by allowing Lunar Assault to cover more of the screen. **Rigwald's Ferocity** and **Rakiata's Flow** are powerful damage supports, but both are expensive and not required to get started.
      
      If you do not have Rakiata's Flow, invest in elemental penetration on the passive tree. **Piercing Claw Damage** and **Dimensional Weak Spot** are alternatives that help overcome enemy elemental resistances.
      
      ### Pounce: Wolves and Predator's Mark
      
      **Brutus' Brain** keeps the wolves alive by making them immortal, although they no longer deal damage. Their value comes from the damage bonuses they provide through **Predator's Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**.
      
      Predator's Mark increases the damage taken by the marked target for each enemy near it. Pack Encouragement grants increased Attack Damage for each minion in your Presence, up to its maximum. Maintaining the wolves therefore improves both the marked-target damage bonus and the build's general attack damage.
      
      The setup also uses **Eternal Mark**, which allows the mark to remain on the enemy after it is detonated. Cross Slash can trigger the mark and create Shocked Ground through **Charged Mark** without permanently losing the benefits of Predator's Mark and Mark of Siphoning.
      
      ### Cross Slash
      
      Cross Slash is the build's finisher and mark detonator. **Elemental Armament** and **Heavy Swing** increase its damage, while **Execute** and **Close Combat** support its role as a high-damage finishing hit.
      
      **Morrigan's Insight** consumes Freeze to increase Cross Slash's damage and grants a Guard benefit. **Biting Frost** is a suitable alternative if you do not have Morrigan's Insight, although you lose the additional defense.
      
      ### Arctic Howl: Empowered Attacks and Living Lightning
      
      Arctic Howl is one of the build's most valuable skills. It empowers attacks, and its repeated cries allow the build to gain additional damage and utility from the war cry setup.
      
      **Echoing Cry** provides multiple cries per activation, while **Tireless** improves the value of empowered attacks. Other supports increase war cry speed, cooldown recovery, and damage. The setup can also debilitate enemies and inflict Critical Weakness, improving both survivability and offensive consistency.
      
      **Living Lightning** is a particularly effective addition. The build gains damage as extra damage of multiple types, including Lightning Damage, which is enough to trigger Living Lightning. Because Arctic Howl activates repeatedly and covers a large area, it can summon multiple lightning minions.
      
      These minions contribute to Pack Encouragement and Predator's Mark, giving the build another way to benefit from maintaining a large number of minions.
      
      ### Lunar Blessing and Other Buffs
      
      Lunar Blessing provides the white Werewolf form and its damaging moonbeams. Cooldown Recovery and Prolonged Duration help with uptime, while Elemental Armament increases its damage. The remaining support slots can be adjusted for damage or area coverage.
      
      The build also uses **Savage Fury** for its damage buff and Onslaught. Put it in the other weapon set so it does not reserve Spirit from the active setup. Activate it when needed, then return to the primary weapon set.
      
      **Berserk** is a core part of the build because it amplifies the benefits of Rage. **Barkskin** is an optional defensive addition if you have enough Spirit. **Vitality** is important for the life regeneration setup, while **Clarity** helps with mana sustain.
      
      You can use different levels of Vitality and Clarity on separate skills to gain their respective benefits, as shown in the Path of Building setup. **Warm Blooded** is also a useful option if you leave your Charm slots empty for the Shaman's Spirit bonus and consequently lack protection against Freeze.
  - title: Gear
    content: |
      The gear prioritizes maximum Rage, damage, attributes, life regeneration, and the Armour and Energy Shield combination. Some items are central to the build, while others are convenient upgrades that can be replaced with well-rolled rares.
      
      ### Helmet: Crown of Eyes
      
      **Crown of Eyes is essential to this version of the build.** Its interaction with Spell Damage is what allows Mystical Rage to provide additional Attack Damage. Replacing it would require reworking the core damage setup.
      
      An additional helmet investment can provide more Energy Shield, but it is optional. Prioritize getting Crown of Eyes first.
      
      ### Talisman
      
      A **Maji Talisman** is a strong choice because it can provide additional maximum Rage. The showcased setup has a relatively low maximum Rage roll, so a better roll offers room to improve.
      
      Look for Melee Skill Levels, Attack Speed, damage, and the attributes needed to equip your gear and skills. This build is attribute-hungry, so Strength and Intelligence can be valuable even when they do not directly increase damage.
      
      The build also uses runes that grant damage as extra damage. One rune provides damage as extra damage of multiple elements, while another provides extra Chaos Damage. The Shaman's rune-related ascendancy interaction adds further extra Physical Damage from the bonded modifier.
      
      ### Amulet
      
      An **Eternal Rage amulet** is highly recommended. It provides additional maximum Rage and frees up Spirit that would otherwise be needed for the Eternal Rage support.
      
      The remaining affixes can provide Spell Damage, maximum Energy Shield, Melee Skill Levels, attributes, and resistances. Spell Damage is particularly useful because Crown of Eyes allows it to contribute to Attack Damage.
      
      A regular amulet can still work if you need to prioritize other stats or cannot afford the Eternal Rage version.
      
      ### Body Armour
      
      **Morior Invictus** is the preferred body armour. Prioritize life regeneration per socketed rune, resistances per socketed rune, and Spirit per socketed rune.
      
      Four sockets are sufficient, although the showcased setup uses five. Use the sockets to address your life regeneration, resistance, and attribute requirements. Maximum Life is another useful option because it can improve recovery effects that scale with maximum Life.
      
      ### Gloves
      
      The showcased gloves immobilize enemies at 50% buildup, making it easier to freeze enemies during mapping and against bosses. This is a convenient option because the build benefits from freezing enemies quickly.
      
      The gloves can also provide life regeneration, melee damage, and maximum Rage through their socketed upgrade, which allows a jewel to be slotted into them. The showcased crafted jewel upgrade reaches **+3 maximum Rage**, but that result requires a specialized crafting process and is not mandatory.
      
      Once you have enough damage and sufficient Freeze buildup, swapping to rare gloves with flat damage and Melee Skill Levels provides a higher damage ceiling.
      
      ### Boots
      
      Prioritize maximum Energy Shield, increased Energy Shield, resistances, life regeneration, and movement speed. The **Iron Slippers** notable grants +2 Armour per 1 Item Energy Shield on your equipped boots, making a pure Energy Shield base particularly useful. It also provides 12% reduced Slowing Potency of Debuffs on you.
      
      Attributes and additional life regeneration are good options if you need them to balance the rest of your equipment.
      
      ### Rings
      
      Flat damage and mana leech are the main priorities. You need at least one source of mana leech on a ring or weapon to sustain the setup that uses **Oisín's Oath**. This support changes Mana Leech from supported skills to recover based on elemental damage types instead of physical damage, so a source of mana leech remains necessary.
      
      Use the remaining affixes for attributes, elemental and Chaos Resistance, and other missing defensive stats. Additional mana-related modifiers can be useful, but they are lower priority once sustain is comfortable.
      
      ### Belt and Flasks
      
      The belt should help round out your defenses and attributes. Armour, Life, Strength, Chaos Resistance, and life regeneration are useful options.
      
      **Lavianga's Spirits** is not required. The showcased build uses it for passive mana recovery, but a regular Mana Flask is a workable alternative.
      
      ### Jewels
      
      Prioritize increased Attack Damage, Spell Damage, Attack Speed, elemental damage, critical damage bonus, life regeneration, Armour, Energy Shield, and maximum Rage.
      
      Spell Damage is especially valuable because of Crown of Eyes. Area Damage and area-related bonuses are also useful because the build's attacks cover large areas.
      
      The build uses crafted upgrades that add maximum Rage and life regeneration. These are strong high-investment options, but ordinary jewels with useful offensive and defensive modifiers can fill the same slots while you work toward them.
  - title: Passive Skill Tree
    content: |
      The passive tree is built around Rage scaling, Heavy Frost, the Armour and Energy Shield combination, and the Shaman's ascendancy interactions. The exact pathing can change with your equipment, but several choices are central to the build.
      
      ### Mystical Rage and Maximum Rage
      
      **Mystical Rage is mandatory.** Each point of Rage grants increased Spell Damage, which Crown of Eyes allows to apply to attacks. Other Rage-related passives provide increased Armour and additional Physical Damage, giving Rage multiple offensive and defensive benefits.
      
      Invest in maximum Rage wherever it is efficient. The tree provides additional maximum Rage while shapeshifted, and the build gains further benefits from its talisman, jewels, and other gear.
      
      ### Heavy Frost
      
      **Heavy Frost is essential.** It grants increased Freeze buildup and allows hits to ignore non-negative elemental resistances of frozen enemies. This is particularly important for Shred, which deals substantial damage against a frozen target.
      
      You can obtain Heavy Frost through a Megalomaniac, an anointment, or another available passive allocation method. The source is flexible, but access to Heavy Frost is not.
      
      ### Zealot's Oath and Life Regeneration
      
      **Zealot's Oath** allows life regeneration to apply to Energy Shield. The tree and gear therefore invest heavily in life regeneration, including bonuses while shapeshifted.
      
      This recovery is important for two reasons: it sustains Energy Shield and helps offset Berserk's life drain. Morior Invictus, socketed runes, and passive tree bonuses all contribute to the recovery setup.
      
      ### Armour and Energy Shield
      
      The tree combines increased Armour and Energy Shield with several interactions that make both defenses more efficient.
      
      **Iron Slippers** grants Armour based on the Item Energy Shield on your equipped boots. This is why the build favors pure Energy Shield boots.
      
      **Insulating Hide** allows 20% of Armour to apply to Elemental Damage while shapeshifted. With the build's high Armour value and Rage scaling, this provides an important additional layer of mitigation.
      
      ### Pack Encouragement
      
      **Pack Encouragement** grants increased Attack Damage for each minion in your Presence, up to its maximum. The immortal wolves from Brutus' Brain and the minions summoned by Living Lightning help the build benefit from this passive.
      
      This is also why Living Lightning is more than a utility choice. Additional minions contribute directly to the build's damage scaling.
      
      ### Pain Attunement and Additional Passive Options
      
      The showcased tree uses **From Nothing** to access Pain Attunement and nearby passive options without spending points on unwanted pathing nodes. This is a tree configuration choice rather than a requirement for the build's core Rage interaction.
      
      The tree also invests in critical damage bonus and reduced critical damage bonus from enemy hits, as well as additional Energy Shield, Armour, life regeneration, damage gained as extra elemental damage, and damage against immobilized enemies.
      
      You can adjust the balance between damage and defense according to your equipment. The build already has substantial damage from Rage stacking, so additional defensive investment remains valuable.
  - title: Shaman Ascendancy
    content: |
      The Shaman's ascendancy choices support the build's defensive layering, Spirit budget, and damage gained from runes and idols.
      
      ### Spirit from Empty Charm Slots: Sacred Flow
      
      **Sacred Flow grants 40 Spirit for each empty Charm slot.** Leaving all three slots empty provides **120 Spirit**, which is a substantial amount for fitting additional buffs and supports.&#x20;
      
      This is optional. If Freeze, Ignite, or other ailments are causing problems, you can equip a Charm and give up some of that Spirit. You still receive a useful bonus from the remaining empty slots.
      
      Warm Blooded can help mitigate Freeze if you prefer to keep the slots empty.
      
      ### Wisdom of the Maji
      
      **Wisdom of the Maji** grants the benefits of Bonded modifiers on your equipped Runes and Idols. Its value depends on the modifiers you use, but the extra damage gained from the rune setup is particularly useful here.
      
      An alternative is **Turning of the Seasons**, which grants Exposure to enemies in your Presence and gives you 10% of Damage as Extra Damage of a random Element. This is a viable offensive option if you prefer it over the additional benefits from Bonded modifiers.
      
      ### Reactive Growth and Avatar of Evolution
      
      **Reactive Growth** grants 10% less Elemental Damage taken and lets you adapt to the highest Elemental Damage Type of each hit you take. Each matching Adaptation grants another 10% less damage taken of that type.
      
      **Avatar of Evolution** shifts 5% of Physical Damage taken to each of Cold, Fire, and Lightning Damage. It also doubles Adaptation effects, although Adaptations last only five seconds.
      
      Together with Armour applying to a portion of elemental damage through Insulating Hide, these effects help make the Shaman exceptionally durable.&#x20;
      
      The remaining ascendancy choices should match your preferred balance of offense and defense.
  - title: High-End Upgrades
    content: |
      The build performs well without expensive gear, but several upgrades can improve its damage, survivability, or convenience.
      
      **Headhunter** is the standout mapping upgrade. Its bonuses can take an already powerful character to another level. Mageblood is another high-investment option if you prefer its flask benefits.
      
      An Eternal Rage amulet frees up Spirit for additional supports. Better maximum Rage rolls on the talisman and socketed upgrades further improve the benefits from Rage. Better Morior Invictus rolls and additional life regeneration can strengthen the defensive setup.
      
      The build can also use additional jewel investment to improve damage, maximum Rage, life regeneration, Armour, and Energy Shield. These are incremental improvements rather than requirements for the build to work.
  - title: Final Thoughts
    content: |
      The Shaman combines several powerful interactions into one cohesive build. Rage increases Attack Damage, Spell Damage, and Armour. Crown of Eyes makes the Spell Damage bonus contribute to attacks, while Berserk amplifies the benefits of Rage. Zealot's Oath, high life regeneration, Armour, and Energy Shield provide a durable defensive foundation.
      
      The result is a fast, hard-hitting Werewolf that remains tanky without relying on mirror-tier equipment. Start with the budget setup, prioritize Crown of Eyes, Mystical Rage, Heavy Frost, and the core defensive interactions, then scale maximum Rage and damage as your gear improves.
