  - title: "Intro"
    content: |
      The Oracle has the highest damage ceiling of any Werewolf ascendancy, thanks to guaranteed critical hits and some unusual passive tree interactions. This build combines that damage with a large pack of wolves and a defensive setup built around Armour, Energy Shield, and life regeneration.

      The budget version is already strong enough for difficult endgame content. You can also invest heavily in critical damage and a low-life setup to push it much further. The Shaman can be tankier, but the Oracle has more room to scale damage.

  - title: "Skills"
    content: |
      The main skill choices focus on three things: getting more value from guaranteed critical hits, maintaining the wolf pack, and making the most of Freeze.

      ### **Shred** and **Lunar Assault**

      **Long Fuse** improves the damage of **Shred**'s ice fragments. For **Lunar Assault**, **Rakiata's Flow** is a major upgrade because it inverts the target's elemental resistances for that skill. Until you have it, use **Cold Penetration** and invest in elemental penetration on the passive tree.

      The setup also uses **Ice Bite**, **Elemental Armament**, **Ancestral Call**, and **Rigwald's Ferocity**. If you can't afford the expensive supports, use suitable Cold or Freeze focused alternatives.

      ### **Pounce**: Wolves and Marks

      **Brutus' Brain** keeps seven wolves around you. They no longer deal damage, but their presence still benefits **Predator's Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**, making them valuable to the build.

      **Eternal Mark** is the important interaction here. It allows the target to remain marked after you detonate the mark with **Cross Slash**. Combined with **Charged Mark**, this lets you create Shocked Ground and Shock the target without giving up the benefits of **Predator's Mark** and **Mark of Siphoning**.

      The setup also uses **Culling Strike**. The wolves can still apply its effect despite dealing no damage.

      ### **Cross Slash**

      **Cross Slash** is both the mark detonator and the main finishing hit. **Elemental Armament** and **Heavy Swing** increase its damage, while **Execute** benefits its role as a finisher.

      **Mórrigan's Insight** consumes Freeze to boost the hit and grants Guard when it does so. **Biting Frost** is a cheaper alternative if you don't have **Mórrigan's Insight**, although you lose that defensive benefit.

      ### **Arctic Howl** and **Living Lightning**

      **Arctic Howl** is especially valuable because the Oracle can reliably trigger effects that require a critical hit. **Blazing Critical** grants damage as extra Fire Damage after critically hitting with the supported skill, making it a strong offensive option here.

      **Echoing Cry** provides multiple cries per use, while **Tireless** improves the benefits from empowered attacks.

      The other important support is **Living Lightning**. The build has a small source of Lightning Damage that can summon an additional minion. This helps scale both **Pack Encouragement** and **Predator's Mark**.

      ### **Lunar Blessing** and Other Skills

      **Lunar Blessing** is a major damage source through its white Werewolf form and moonbeams. Cooldown Recovery helps maintain uptime, and Elemental Armament improves the moonbeams. Since the build uses **Blood Magic**, **Efficiency** reduces the skill's Life cost. Use **Prolonged Duration** if you prefer a longer duration.

      **Herald of Ice** uses the Oracle's **Dangerous Blossom** interaction for an armour-break setup. Armour breaks can trigger explosions that help Freeze enemies and chain further **Herald of Ice** explosions. **Magnified Area** and Elemental Armament support this setup.

      **Berserk** benefits from the build's large Rage pool, while **Eternal Rage** helps sustain the Rage needed for **Lunar Blessing**. **Vitality** supports add Life Regeneration. Keep **Savage Fury** in the other weapon set so you can activate it without reserving Spirit from your main setup.
  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      The main priorities are critical hit chance and damage, Life Regeneration, and the Armour and Energy Shield interactions from the passive tree. You don't need every expensive item to get the core build running.

      ### Body Armour

      **Morior Invictus** is a key item. Prioritize Life Regeneration, Resistances, and Spirit per socketed rune. Four sockets are enough, although the showcased build uses five.

      Life Regeneration is particularly valuable because **Zealot's Oath** makes it recover Energy Shield instead of Life.

      ### Gloves

      **Sine Aequo** immobilizes enemies at 50% buildup, making it easier to reach the buildup needed for Freeze and immobilization.

      Rare gloves with **+2 to Melee Skill Levels** and flat damage can provide a higher damage ceiling, but you'll need to compensate for the lost buildup. Once your damage is high enough, rare damage gloves become more attractive.

      ### Talisman

      A **Spiny Talisman** is the preferred base because of its high critical hit potential. Aim for a weapon critical hit chance roll above 13.5%, with 14% or higher as a strong target. Better rolls are possible, but they become increasingly expensive.

      Melee Skill Levels and physical damage are also valuable. A base with more maximum Rage is an alternative, although the showcased build already has a large Rage pool and maintaining it can become inconvenient.

      ### Amulet, Rings, and Belt

      A rare amulet can provide Spirit, maximum Energy Shield, Melee Skill Levels, Resistances, and increased Energy Shield from equipped body armour.

      An **Eternal Rage** amulet is an alternative for the more expensive setups. It frees up 100 Spirit for additional skills and supports.

      For rings, prioritize flat damage, Life Regeneration, Resistances, and Attributes. The belt is another source of Life Regeneration, Armour, Life, Strength, and Chaos Resistance. **Mageblood** and **Headhunter** are possible high-end upgrades, not requirements for the budget build.

      ### Helmet and Boots

      These slots take advantage of two passive tree interactions that let the build scale Armour and Energy Shield together.

      Use a high-Armour helmet because the relevant passive grants maximum Energy Shield based on the Armour on your equipped helmet. For boots, prioritize Energy Shield because the corresponding passive grants Armour based on the Energy Shield on your equipped boots. Pure Armour and pure Energy Shield bases make the most of these interactions.

      Look for Life, Attributes, Resistances, and Life Regeneration on the helmet. On the boots, prioritize Energy Shield, Resistances, Life Regeneration, and Movement Speed.

      **Legacy of Wanderlust** is a useful upgrade because it makes your speed unaffected by slows. Effects that reduce attack speed are particularly disruptive to this build. A **Dousing Charm** helps prevent Ignite.

      ### Mirror-Tier Upgrades: Low Life

      The mirror-tier version uses a low-life setup to add more damage through supports and passive choices. These are optional upgrades for pushing the build further.

      **Atziri's Communion** helps establish the low-life state when activating a skill that reserves a large amount of Spirit. This opens up additional low-life bonuses.

      **Direstrike** grants increased Attack Damage while on low life. The showcased setup places it on separate skills to benefit from multiple instances. **Execute** also adds damage with hits against low-life enemies, so the high-investment version uses it on **Shred**, **Lunar Assault**, **Cross Slash**, **Lunar Blessing**, and **Herald of Ice**.

      **Pain Attunement** adds Critical Damage Bonus while on low life. The setup is designed to remain below the relevant threshold, so this is a consistent offensive benefit.

      The build can also use **Mystical Rage** to gain increased Spell Damage per point of Rage. **Crown of Eyes** allows that Spell Damage to contribute to Attack Damage. This replaces the Armour-focused helmet setup, so you give up some of the defensive benefits in exchange for more damage. An **Eternal Rage** amulet is recommended to free up Spirit for the additional supports.

      Other expensive options include:

      - **Garukhan's Resolve**: A high-end **Shred** support that can double the Critical Damage Bonus when both critical hit rolls succeed. Guaranteed critical hits make this particularly powerful.
      - **Rite of Passage**: Adds Energy Shield, damage, and damage gained as extra Cold Damage in the showcased setup.
      - **Voices**: Provides additional jewel sockets for further investment in damage and defenses.
      - **Mageblood** or **Headhunter**: Choose based on whether you prefer flask benefits or kill-based bonuses.

      These upgrades are expensive and unnecessary for the build to function. The budget version is a strong starting point, and the low-life setup gives you another way to scale once you're ready to invest heavily.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      The Oracle's passive tree is the main reason this build can scale so far. The key choices combine guaranteed critical hits, access to otherwise unavailable passives, and a defensive setup that turns Life Regeneration into sustained Energy Shield recovery.

      ### **Inevitable Critical Hits**

      **Inevitable Critical Hits** guarantees critical hits by rerolling a hit's critical outcome until it succeeds. Each reroll comes with a penalty to Critical Damage Bonus, so critical hit chance still matters.

      More critical hit chance reduces the number of rerolls needed and preserves more damage. Aim for roughly **30% to 50% critical hit chance**. The showcased build has 41%, which is a good target. After that, invest in Critical Damage Bonus to increase the damage of your guaranteed critical hits.

      ### **Paths Not Taken** and **Entwined Realities**

      **Paths Not Taken** reveals additional passive options, while **Entwined Realities** lets you allocate nodes that aren't connected to your main tree, provided they fall within range of a selected Keystone.

      These options give the Oracle access to useful combinations of Attributes, Skill Speed, Life Regeneration, Armour, Energy Shield, Critical Damage Bonus, and damage. They also provide access to additional maximum Rage, which improves both attack damage and **Lunar Blessing** uptime.

      The showcased tree prioritizes defenses through these options rather than using the **Crown of Eyes** damage setup. You can shift toward more offensive choices as your gear improves.

      ### **Heavy Frost**

      **Heavy Frost** is essential. It allows hits to ignore non-negative elemental resistances on frozen enemies, making **Shred** particularly effective after **Lunar Assault** freezes the target.

      You can obtain it through a **Megalomaniac**, an amulet anointment, or another available source. The exact route depends on your tree and gear, but make sure you have access to it.

      ### **Pack Encouragement**

      **Pack Encouragement** grants increased Attack Damage for each minion in your presence, up to its maximum. **Brutus' Brain** maintains seven wolves, and **Living Lightning** can summon another minion.

      This also complements **Predator's Mark**, so keeping the wolves around improves both your general attack damage and your damage against marked targets.

      ### **Zealot's Oath** and Life Regeneration

      **Zealot's Oath** makes Life Regeneration apply to Energy Shield. The build invests heavily in regeneration so that it can recover Energy Shield continuously rather than relying entirely on recharge.

      Another passive interaction converts Arcane Surge's benefit into Life Regeneration. The setup also uses **Mind over Matter** alongside **Blood Magic** to access the passive interactions it needs. With **Blood Magic**, skills cost Life instead of Mana, and the build's substantial Life Regeneration helps cover those costs.

      ### Armour and Energy Shield

      The tree reinforces the defensive setup with Armour and Energy Shield bonuses. Rage also grants increased Armour, and **Berserk** amplifies the benefits gained from Rage. Another passive allows a portion of Armour to apply to elemental damage.

      Together with **Zealot's Oath**, these interactions provide the build's defensive foundation. The showcased tree leans toward survivability, but you can move points into physical damage, Critical Damage Bonus, and other offensive bonuses if you want a more aggressive version.
  - title: "Final Thoughts"
    content: |
      The Oracle combines guaranteed critical hits with passive tree flexibility that opens up some powerful offensive and defensive interactions. The result is a Werewolf with a high damage ceiling, strong minion scaling, and a durable Armour and Energy Shield setup.

      Start with the budget build, then invest in critical damage and the low-life setup if you want to push it further. The expensive version has plenty of room to grow, but the core build is already strong without mirror-tier gear.
