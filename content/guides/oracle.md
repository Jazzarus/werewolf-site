  - title: Intro
    content: |
      The Oracle is the strongest ascendancy for a Werewolf build in Path of Exile 2. Guaranteed critical hits give it an exceptionally high damage ceiling, but the Oracle's appeal goes beyond raw damage. Its unique passive tree options open up some genuinely interesting builds, combining unusual Keystone interactions, additional passive nodes, and powerful defensive and offensive synergies that other ascendancies cannot reproduce in the same way.
      
      This build takes advantage of those options to achieve high damage while remaining surprisingly tanky. It combines guaranteed critical hits, a large pack of wolves, and a defensive setup built around Armour, Energy Shield, and life regeneration. The budget version is already capable of handling difficult endgame content, with plenty of room to scale further through expensive gear and specialized passive tree choices.
      
      The main tradeoff is that the Shaman can be tankier, but the Oracle has a much higher damage ceiling. If you enjoy experimenting with unusual passive tree interactions while building a powerful Werewolf, this is an especially interesting ascendancy to play.

  - title: Skill Gems and Support Gems
    content: |
      The standard Werewolf setup remains largely intact. The Oracle-specific choices focus on increasing damage through guaranteed critical hits, scaling the number of minions around you, and getting more value from Arctic Howl and the build's Freeze interactions.
      
      ### Shred and Lunar Assault
      
      Shred uses the usual attack speed and damage supports, with Long Fuse increasing the damage of its ice fragments.
      
      Lunar Assault is especially important because it is supported by **Rakiata's Flow**, which inverts the target's elemental resistances for the supported skill. This is a major damage upgrade. Before obtaining Rakiata's Flow, use Cold Penetration and invest in elemental penetration on the passive tree.
      
      The setup also uses Ice Bite, Elemental Armament, Ancestral Call, and Rigwald's Ferocity. Ancestral Call helps with clear, while the other supports contribute to the build's damage and Freeze-focused setup. If you do not have the expensive supports yet, use suitable Cold- or Freeze-focused alternatives.
      
      ### Pounce: Wolves and Mark Scaling
      
      This build uses **Brutus' Brain** to maintain seven wolves. Their presence improves the build's damage through Predator's Mark and the Pack Encouragement passive.
      
      The key support interaction is **Eternal Mark**. It allows an enemy to remain marked after the mark is detonated, so you can trigger the mark with Cross Slash, create Shocked Ground through Charged Mark, and still benefit from Predator's Mark and Mark of Siphoning afterward.
      
      This makes the opening of the damage rotation particularly effective: Pounce applies the mark, and Cross Slash triggers it to create Shocked Ground and Shock the target without permanently losing the mark.
      
      The setup also uses Culling Strike. Even though the wolves deal no damage with Brutus' Brain, they can still apply the support's effect.
      
      ### Cross Slash: The Finisher
      
      Cross Slash is supported to maximize its damage as an execution skill. Elemental Armament and Heavy Swing increase its damage, while **Morrigan's Insight** consumes Freeze to boost the hit and grants Guard when it does so.
      
      Biting Frost is an alternative if you do not have Morrigan's Insight. The latter's additional defensive benefit is useful, but the main purpose of both options is to increase the damage of Cross Slash against a frozen target.
      
      ### Arctic Howl: More Damage and More Minions
      
      Arctic Howl is particularly valuable in this build because it empowers attacks and provides another way to benefit from the Oracle's guaranteed critical hits.
      
      Echoing Cry provides multiple cries per use, while Tireless increases the benefits from empowered attacks. **Living Lightning** is another important choice: the build's small source of Lightning Damage can trigger an additional minion, which contributes to the damage scaling from Pack Encouragement and Predator's Mark.
      
      Blazing Critical is a strong offensive support here. It grants damage as extra Fire Damage after critically hitting with the supported skill. Because of the Oracle's guaranteed critical hits, Arctic Howl reliably activates this effect.
      
      ### Lunar Blessing and Spirit Setup
      
      Lunar Blessing remains a priority because its white Werewolf form and moonbeams contribute heavily to the build's damage. Cooldown Recovery helps maintain uptime, while Elemental Armament improves the moonbeams. Efficiency reduces the life cost of the skill because the build uses Blood Magic. Prolonged Duration is an alternative if you prefer a more comfortable duration.
      
      Herald of Ice uses the Oracle's Dangerous Blossoms interaction to support an armour-break setup. Armour breaks can trigger explosions that help Freeze enemies and chain further Herald of Ice explosions across the screen. Magnified Area and Elemental Armament support this approach.
      
      Berserk takes advantage of the build's large Rage pool, while Eternal Rage helps sustain the Rage needed for Lunar Blessing. Vitality supports add life regeneration, which is particularly valuable for the defensive setup. Savage Fury remains in the other weapon set so it can be activated without reserving Spirit from the active setup.
  - title: Gear
    content: |
      The gear prioritizes critical hit chance and damage, life regeneration, attributes and resistances, and the Armour and Energy Shield interactions used by the passive tree.
      
      ### Body Armour
      
      **Morior Invictus** is a key item. Prioritize life regeneration per socketed rune, resistances per socketed rune, and Spirit per socketed rune. Four sockets are sufficient for the setup, although the showcased build uses five.
      
      The runes should help meet your life regeneration, attribute, and resistance requirements. Life regeneration is especially important because Zealot's Oath allows it to recover Energy Shield.
      
      ### Gloves
      
      The showcased gloves immobilize enemies at 50% buildup, making Freeze and immobilization easier to achieve without requiring as much additional investment.
      
      Rare gloves with +2 to Melee Skill Levels and flat damage can offer a higher damage ceiling, but you will need other sources of Freeze buildup to compensate. The showcased gloves are the convenient option; rare damage gloves are worth considering if you want to push the build further. Once you're very geared and have a lot of damage, you can use the rare gloves without needing extra buildup because the damage itself will compensate.
      
      ### Talisman
      
      A **Spiny Talisman** is the preferred base because the build benefits greatly from critical hit chance and critical damage bonus. Aim for a high critical hit chance roll on the weapon, ideally above 13.5%, with 14% or more as a strong target. Higher rolls are possible but become increasingly expensive.
      
      Melee Skill Levels and physical damage are also valuable offensive stats. A base that grants more Rage is an alternative, but the showcased build already has a large Rage pool, and maintaining that much Rage can become inconvenient.
      
      ### Amulet, Rings, and Belt
      
      A rare amulet can provide Spirit, maximum Energy Shield, Melee Skill Levels, resistances, and increased Energy Shield from equipped body armour.
      
      An **Eternal Rage amulet** is an alternative that frees up 100 Spirit for additional skills or supports. This is especially useful in the more heavily invested versions.
      
      For rings, prioritize flat damage, life regeneration, resistances, and attributes. The belt is another useful source of life regeneration, alongside Armour, Life, Strength, and Chaos Resistance. Expensive options such as Mageblood or Headhunter are possible upgrades, but they are not required for the budget version.
      
      ### Helmet and Boots
      
      The helmet and boots take advantage of two passive tree interactions that let the build scale Armour and Energy Shield together.
      
      The helmet should prioritize **Armour**, because the relevant passive grants maximum Energy Shield based on the Armour on your equipped helmet. The boots should prioritize **Energy Shield**, because their corresponding passive grants Armour based on the Energy Shield on your equipped boots.
      
      This means a pure-Armour helmet and pure-Energy-Shield boots can provide both defenses more efficiently than using hybrid bases. Look for life, attributes, resistances, and life regeneration on the helmet, and Energy Shield, resistances, life regeneration, and movement speed on the boots.
      
      **Legacy of the Wanderlust** is a useful upgrade because it makes your speed unaffected by slows. Effects that reduce attack speed are particularly disruptive to Werewolf gameplay. The build also uses a Dousing Charm to prevent Ignite.
  - title: Passive Skill Tree
    content: |
      The passive tree is one of the biggest reasons to play Oracle. Its unique mechanics allow you to combine guaranteed critical hits with unusual Keystone interactions, additional passive options, minion scaling, and a defensive setup that would be difficult to reproduce on other ascendancies.
      
      ### Inevitable Critical Hits
      
      **Inevitable Critical Hits** is the defining Oracle node. It allows hits to reroll their critical hit outcome until they critically hit, with a penalty to critical damage bonus for each reroll.
      
      Critical hit chance therefore remains important even though the build guarantees critical hits. More critical hit chance reduces the number of rerolls needed and helps preserve critical damage bonus. The showcased setup aims for approximately 30% to 50% critical hit chance, with 41% in the example. After reaching a suitable amount, invest in critical damage bonus to increase the payoff from each critical hit.
      
      ### Paths Not Taken and Entwined Realities
      
      **Paths Not Taken** reveals additional passive options that are not normally available in the tree. **Entwined Realities** allows the build to allocate nodes that are not connected to the main tree, provided they fall within the range of a selected Keystone.
      
      These mechanics create a large amount of flexibility and enable some of the most distinctive parts of the build. The showcased tree uses them to reach useful bonuses to attributes, skill speed, life regeneration, Armour, Energy Shield, critical damage bonus, and damage.
      
      The Oracle also has access to additional maximum Rage through these options. That improves both attack damage and Lunar Blessing uptime, making Rage scaling valuable for offense and utility.
      
      ### Heavy Frost
      
      **Heavy Frost** is essential. It allows hits to ignore non-negative elemental resistances of frozen enemies, which is particularly powerful when Shred attacks a target that has been frozen by Lunar Assault.
      
      You can obtain Heavy Frost through a Megalomaniac, an amulet anointment, or another available source. The exact route depends on your gear and tree, but make sure the build has access to this node.
      
      ### Pack Encouragement and Minion Scaling
      
      **Pack Encouragement** grants increased attack damage for each minion in your presence, up to its maximum. Brutus' Brain helps maintain seven wolves, while Living Lightning can summon additional minions.
      
      The same minion count also improves Predator's Mark, making this interaction a major part of the build's damage. It is one of the reasons the build invests in maintaining its wolves rather than treating them as incidental summons.
      
      ### Zealot's Oath and Life Regeneration
      
      The defensive setup revolves around **Zealot's Oath**, which allows life regeneration to apply to Energy Shield. The tree invests heavily in life regeneration and uses additional passive interactions to increase it further.
      
      One of these interactions converts Arcane Surge's benefit into life regeneration through the relevant passive setup. The build uses Mind over Matter alongside Blood Magic, making the usual mana-based defensive benefit irrelevant while retaining access to the passive interactions it needs.
      
      This results in a large amount of continuous Energy Shield recovery rather than relying entirely on recharge. It is a major part of how the build stays tanky despite its offensive investment.
      
      ### Armour, Energy Shield, and Blood Magic
      
      The tree reinforces the hybrid defenses with bonuses to Armour and Energy Shield. It also takes advantage of Rage granting increased Armour, with Berserk further amplifying the benefits gained from Rage. Another passive allows a portion of Armour to apply to elemental damage.
      
      **Blood Magic** solves the build's mana problems by making skills cost Life instead of Mana. With the amount of life regeneration in the build, skill costs are manageable, and you no longer need to dedicate as much effort to mana sustain.
      
      ### Balancing Damage and Defense
      
      The showcased tree prioritizes survivability. It takes additional Armour, Energy Shield, and life regeneration, alongside offensive bonuses such as damage gained as extra elemental damage and increased damage against immobilized enemies.
      
      You can move points out of defensive investments and allocate them toward physical damage, critical damage bonus, and other offensive bonuses if you prefer a more aggressive setup. The Oracle's passive tree flexibility makes this an appealing option for players who want to experiment with different configurations.
  - title: Mirror-Tier Upgrades
    content: |
      The mirror-tier version adds a low-life damage setup and several expensive upgrades. These are optional investments for players who want to push the build's damage ceiling further.
      
      ### Atziri's Communion and Low Life
      
      **Atziri's Communion** is used to establish the low-life state when activating a skill that reserves a large amount of Spirit. This enables additional bonuses that improve damage while on low life.
      
      **Direstrike** grants increased attack damage while on low life. By placing it on separate skills, the build can benefit from multiple instances of the support. **Execute** provides additional damage with hits while on low life, so the showcased configuration adds it to Shred, Lunar Assault, Cross Slash, Lunar Blessing, and Herald of Ice.
      
      The Oracle can also take **Pain Attunement** for increased critical damage bonus while on low life. This is more attractive in this setup because the build remains below the relevant threshold, avoiding the drawback associated with being on full life.
      
      ### Crown of Eyes
      
      The low-life version can also use **Mystical Rage**, which grants increased Spell Damage for each point of Rage. This opens up **Crown of Eyes**, allowing Spell Damage to contribute to Attack Damage.
      
      This is an offensive alternative to the original helmet setup. You give up the Energy Shield benefits from the Armour-focused helmet interaction, so this tradeoff is best suited to a heavily invested character.
      
      An Eternal Rage amulet is recommended for this version because the freed Spirit can be used for additional low-life supports and other effects.
      
      ### Further High-Investment Options
      
      - **Garukhan's Resolve:** A high-end support for Shred that can double the critical damage bonus when both critical hit rolls succeed. Because the Oracle guarantees critical hits, this is a powerful way to increase Shred's damage.
      - **Rite of Passage:** The showcased option provides increased Energy Shield, increased damage, and damage gained as extra Cold Damage.
      - **Headhunter or Mageblood:** Both are possible upgrades, depending on whether you prefer Headhunter's kill-based bonuses or Mageblood's flask benefits.
      - **Voices:** Provides access to additional jewel sockets, allowing more investment into damage, life regeneration, Armour, and other useful stats.
      - **Additional Spirit supports:** The Spirit freed by the amulet can be used for further offensive and defensive effects, including supports that improve damage against nearby enemies or help mitigate incoming hits.
      
      These upgrades are expensive and unnecessary for the build to function. The budget setup remains a strong starting point, with the mirror-tier version offering further ways to scale damage and defenses.
  - title: Final Thoughts
    content: |
      The Oracle stands out for two reasons: guaranteed critical hits and the unusual passive tree configurations available through its ascendancy mechanics. Together, they enable a Werewolf build with a high damage ceiling, strong minion scaling, and a defensive setup built around Armour, Energy Shield, and life regeneration.
      
      The budget version already offers strong endgame performance. With further investment, the low-life setup and high-end supports can push its damage considerably higher.
      
      If you enjoy experimenting with passive tree interactions and want a Werewolf that combines powerful damage with a durable defensive foundation, the Oracle is not only an excellent choice, but the best choice.
