      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Oracle**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Oracle** is the best werewolf ascendancy. It is a druid, and the werewolf was meant to be played by the druid. Every ascendancy I’ve built a werewolf on will take you where you need to go, but the **Oracle**’s damage ceiling is unmatched, thanks to its guaranteed critical strikes. The **Shaman** is also a druid ascendancy and a bit tankier, but the **Oracle**’s damage leaves it behind, and I’m happy to give up that tankiness for the damage.

      This guide covers the budget version, which is very tanky, easy to afford, and still beats everything in the game. The stats are the usual werewolf ones: attack speed, elemental damage, critical chance, and critical bonus. You are guaranteed to crit as an **Oracle**, but a little critical chance still helps, and the passive tree section explains why. The build is hybrid armour and energy shield. **Zealot’s Oath** makes life regeneration apply to energy shield, and armour also applies to elemental damage, which is where the tankiness comes from. If you keep investing, the ceiling is infinite, and the Mirror Tier section at the end shows where that leads.

      The rotation is the [same as in the general guide](/werewolf-general/#rotation), with one addition. Activate **Savage Fury** whenever it reaches 100%. You don’t need it in easy content, but in something like a fully delirious map you will want it.

    content: |
      **Shred** and **Lunar Assault** use the standard setups from the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups), with the lineage supports on **Lunar Assault**. Everything below is what changes or what the **Oracle** adds.

      **Pounce**. I use **Brutus’ Brain** because we want our wolves with us at all times. We benefit from having many minions. **Predator’s Mark** deals more damage the more creatures are around the target, and we always have seven wolves. **Pack Encouragement**, which I cover in the passive tree section, adds to that. Even though **Brutus’ Brain** makes the wolves deal zero damage, they can still apply **Culling Strike II**. The one change from the [general setup](/werewolf-general/#setup-pounce) is **Mark of Siphoning** in place of **Mark for Death II**. **Eternal Mark** keeps the target marked after we detonate the mark, so we keep the benefits of both **Predator’s Mark** and **Mark of Siphoning**.

      **Cross Slash**. For the freeze consumption I use **Morrigan’s Insight**, which works like **Biting Frost II** and also gives you 1400 guard when it consumes the freeze. That is a bit of extra tankiness, and it comes in handy while mapping. If you don’t have it, **Biting Frost II** does the job without the guard.

      **Arctic Howl**. The setup matches the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#setup-arctic-howl), and two of its supports work better on the **Oracle**. **Living Lightning II** summons a small lightning minion whenever you hit something with lightning damage. The werewolf has many sources of damage, and even a tiny amount like 10 to 14 lightning damage is enough to trigger it. We want those extra minions for **Predator’s Mark** and **Pack Encouragement**. **Blazing Critical** gives your attacks 15% of damage as fire damage for 5 seconds when you critically hit with the supported skill. An **Oracle** always crits, so every **Arctic Howl** gives you that fire damage on all your attacks.

      **Lunar Blessing**. This is the one place where I deviate from the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#setup-lunar-blessing). I use **Efficiency** instead of **Prolonged Duration**. **Lunar Blessing** is a very costly skill, and since we use **Blood Magic**, it costs life instead of mana, so I don’t want to lose much life when I cast it. If you want more comfort, you can run **Prolonged Duration** in that slot.

      **Herald of Ice**. An **Oracle** is guaranteed to get **Dangerous Blossom**, so you can always use the [armour break setup](/werewolf-general/#setup-herald-of-ice) from the general guide, with **Armour Break III** and **Armour Explosion**. **Herald of Ice** breaks armour, the armour explosion can freeze and chain **Herald of Ice** again, and you get a huge chain reaction of explosions across the screen.

      **Spirit gems**. **Berserk** is in because we run a lot of rage, 82 in this build. **Berserk** takes away life, and since **Zealot’s Oath** applies life regeneration to energy shield, we want a lot of life regeneration anyway. That is why I run **Vitality II**, to recover the life **Berserk** takes. I put **Vitality II** on one spirit gem and **Vitality I** on another, and we get the benefit of both. **Savage Fury** goes in the other weapon set, and I put **Prolonged Duration** on it so the buff lasts longer when I use it.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[oracle-full-endgame]

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. Roll life regeneration per socket filled, elemental resistances per socket filled, and spirit per socket filled. Mine has five sockets, but four is more than enough, and the stats matter more than the socket count. Fill the sockets with what you need. I needed attributes and lightning resistance, so I used strength and lightning resistance, plus life regeneration. The best one is **Tecrod’s Gaze**, which regenerates 1.5% of maximum life per second. That regeneration applies to our energy shield, so it makes the build comfortable and very tanky.

      **Gloves**. I always recommend **Sine Aequo** on a werewolf build. It makes you immobilise enemies at 50% buildup instead of 100%, so you freeze them much faster. It does technically lower your damage. Eventually, rare gloves with plus two levels to melee skills and flat damage beat **Sine Aequo**, and the damage ceiling is insane. The catch is that you then need other sources of freeze buildup, like **Overwhelming Presence**. It never matches **Sine Aequo**, but you get close. I like **Sine Aequo** enough that I don’t think about it and just use it. My gloves have a jewel socket from **Cadigan’s Epiphany**, and the jewel in it rolls life regeneration, attack damage, maximum rage, and armour.

      **Weapon**. **Spiny Talisman**. Some people will say to use a **Maji Talisman** for more rage, but over 80 rage you get diminishing returns. It is a pain to refill quickly, you don’t get the full benefit all the time, and it gets annoying. The **Oracle** wants some critical chance and a lot of critical bonus, so the **Spiny Talisman** is the best base. Get one with critical hit chance, with the weapon’s critical chance over 13.5% and ideally over 14%. Mine is 14.4%. You can go over 15%, but that is very expensive. The rest is the typical weapon stats, like levels of melee skills and physical damage.

      **Amulet**. I use a rare amulet with spirit, maximum energy shield, levels of melee skills, elemental resistances, fire resistance, and increased energy shield from equipped body armour. You can also use an **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**, which frees up 100 spirit. You could spend that on **Overwhelming Presence**, and then you can change your gloves.

      **Rings**. On one ring I want flat damage, life regeneration, and resistances. The other has flat damage, attributes, chaos resistance, life regeneration, and life. Flat damage and life regeneration matter most in this build, but you also need to solve your attributes and resistances.

      **Belt**. Armour, life, strength, chaos resistance, and life regeneration. The belt is a very good source of life regeneration. If you can afford a **Mageblood** or **Headhunter**, use one.

      **Helmet**. The **Ruinic Helm** node gives maximum energy shield based on the armour on your equipped helmet, so you want a full armour helmet. Mine has 1,800 armour, and **Ruinic Helm** turns that into about 900 energy shield. The rest of the stats are armour, life, intelligence, a resistance, and life regeneration.

      **Boots**. The **Iron Slippers** node gives plus 2 armour per 1 energy shield on your equipped boots, so you want full energy shield boots. Not hybrid, and not pure armour. Mine give almost 700 armour on top of 300 energy shield, with fire resistance, chaos resistance, life regeneration per second, and movement speed. The rune sockets on both pieces are flexible. You can use them to fix attributes or resistances, or use an **Iron Rune** for more energy shield and armour.

      **Legacy of Wanderlust**. I use it because I had the money to buy it, and it is very good if you can get one. Your speed is unaffected by slows. One of the werewolf’s biggest weaknesses is anything that slows attack speed, like **Temporal Chains** and shocked ground. Movement speed slows don’t matter, but we need to attack fast.

      **Charms**. Because we use **Blood Magic**, there is no mana flask. Ignite is very annoying for this build, so I run a **Dousing Charm** to prevent it.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      As an **Oracle**, you always pick the same ascendancy nodes. Start with **Inevitable Critical Hits**, which makes every hit you deal a crit. If a hit doesn’t crit, it rerolls to see if it crits, and each reroll gives 30% less critical bonus. That is why you still want some critical chance, since the sooner you crit, the fewer rerolls you pay for. I already did the math, so you don’t have to. The sweet spot for critical chance is between 30% and 50%, and I’m at 41%. After that, take all the critical bonus you can get. Take **The Lesser Harm** as well, which makes you a bit tankier.

      On the other side, take **The Unseen Path**, which reveals the **Oracle**-only nodes that aren’t normally in the tree. Then take **Entwined Realities**, one of my favorite things in the game. It lets you allocate nodes that aren’t connected to your tree if they are near a keystone you allocated. **From Nothing** is similar, but it lets you skip taking the keystone. With **Entwined Realities** you have to take the keystone, but the range is bigger and you can have as many as you want. **From Nothing** has a smaller range, and you can only have one. I use several **Entwined Realities**.

      I start from the energy shield side of the tree. I’m not using **Crown of Eyes**, so I don’t take the spell damage and went with tankiness instead. **Anger Management** gives plus 15 maximum rage, which is a lot of damage. I have a **Megalomaniac** with **Heavy Frost** on it. You need **Heavy Frost** somewhere, and it doesn’t have to be a **Megalomaniac**. An anoint on your amulet works too. I also take **Iron Slippers** and **Ruinic Helm** for the gear covered above.

      **Zealot’s Oath** is very important for this build, and since we have it, **Entwined Realities** lets us take **Pack Encouragement** near it. **Pack Encouragement** gives 5% increased attack damage for each minion in your presence, up to a maximum of 80%. Our seven wolves give us 35%, so there is room to go higher, and **Living Lightning II** helps fill it. I also take **Ancestral Bond**. We don’t use totems, so it has no downside, and it gives me access to the nodes around it.

      For life regeneration, I take **Elemental Equilibrium** to reach **Sanguimantic Rituals**. Without it the path is four nodes, and with it only two. **Sanguimantic Rituals** regenerates 1% of maximum life per second, and it makes Arcane Surge grant more life regeneration rate instead of mana regeneration rate. We get Arcane Surge from **Mind over Matter** and the two nodes in its range, which give Arcane Surge on critical hits and increased Arcane Surge effect. Everything we do crits, and there is so much happening at once, with wolves, howls, assaults, and Pounces, that we effectively always have Arcane Surge. **Mind over Matter** itself does nothing for us, since **Blood Magic** means we have no mana to take damage from, and we don’t care about the mana recovery penalty. Arcane Surge turns into life regeneration through **Sanguimantic Rituals**, and my life regeneration goes from 458 to over 550, maybe 600. Because of **Zealot’s Oath**, it applies to energy shield too. When you get hit, you get back up right away. It is constant regeneration, and you never wait for a recharge.

      **Blood Magic** means mana is a solved problem. With **Zealot’s Oath** and all that regeneration, we don’t think about the cost of skills at all. On my other werewolf builds, solving mana is always a pain. On the **Oracle**, you take **Blood Magic**, get a pile of life regeneration, and forget about it. In range of **Blood Magic** is **Vengeful Fury**, which makes every rage grant 1% increased armour. With 82 rage that is 82% increased armour, and **Berserk** pushes it over 100%.

      I like being tanky, so I took the defensive nodes. If you prefer a glass cannon, drop them and take more damage nodes. There are many ways to increase damage and not many ways to increase defenses, which is why I went defensive.
  - title: "Mirror Tier Build"
    content: |
      This version goes for as much damage as possible.

      **Low life**. **Atziri’s Communion** is very expensive. Put it on a skill that costs a lot of spirit, and it makes that skill reserve life instead, which guarantees you are on low life. Being on low life gives you many modifiers that increase your damage. **Direstrike II** costs 40 spirit and gives 70% increased attack damage while on low life, across all your skills. **Direstrike I** on a different skill stacks with it, for 120% in total. **Execute III** makes supported skills deal 80% more damage with hits while you are on low life, so put it on **Shred**, **Lunar Assault**, **Lunar Blessing**, and **Herald of Ice**. **Cross Slash** already has it.

      Since you are an **Oracle**, you can also take **Pain Attunement**, which gives 30% more critical damage bonus on low life. I normally skip it, because it also gives 30% less critical damage bonus on full life. Constantly on low life, it is extra critical bonus. It also opens up the nodes in its range, so I take **Mystical Rage**, which makes every rage grant 2% increased spell damage. That opens the door to **Crown of Eyes**, which converts spell damage into attack damage. With all that rage, you get even more damage. If you do this, drop **Ruinic Helm**, since you are no longer getting energy shield from your helmet’s armour.

      I recommend the **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage** in this setup, to free up spirit for **Direstrike I** and **Direstrike II**, **Vitality**, and everything else. You can also use **Her Declaration** and **Seraph’s Heart**. For 45 spirit, **Seraph’s Heart** gives enemy hits a 20% chance to treat all your resistances as 90%, which is good for tankiness. **Her Declaration** intimidates enemies in your presence, so they take 10% increased damage and deal 10% reduced damage. That works offensively and defensively.

      **More upgrades**. **Rite of Passage**: Spirit of the Owl is one of the cheapest golden charms. It gives 60% increased energy shield, 80% increased damage, and gain 20% of damage as extra cold damage. That makes you tankier and adds damage.

      The best support gem for this build is **Garukhan’s Resolve**. It sets the maximum critical hit chance of supported skills to 50%, and your critical hit chance is rolled twice. If either roll succeeds, the hit crits and the critical damage bonus applies as normal. If both succeed, you deal double the critical damage bonus. Since you are always critting, you apply that double critical bonus when using it. Put it in **Shred**, since that is your skill for hitting bosses hard.

      **Headhunter** is very good for wolf builds, because it lets you go very fast. You kill everything, get the bonuses, and keep going. A **Mageblood** works too, but I like the modifiers from **Headhunter**. With **Voices** you get two more jewel sockets, which means more damage, life regeneration, and armour. The ceiling is infinite, and you can keep upgrading every slot. The budget version is already the strongest werewolf.
