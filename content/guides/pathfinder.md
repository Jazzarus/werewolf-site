      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Pathfinder**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Pathfinder** is a ranger ascendancy, and I like it more than the **Deadeye**. It is a very well-rounded werewolf: it has enough damage, enough tankiness, and enough speed. The **Deadeye** is faster, but the **Pathfinder** is more consistent, and I would place it above the **Deadeye**. The rotation is the one from the general guide, and nothing changes in it.

      The ascendancy is the reason. Some classes only give you one or two meaningful choices and the rest are points you spend because you have to spend them. On the **Pathfinder** every choice is meaningful: one is for damage, one is for defence, and one is for both. **Relentless Pursuit** is one of the main reasons I like it. Your speed is unaffected by slows, and slows are one of the biggest weaknesses of the werewolf. We go in face to face and we have to swipe to deal our damage, so being slowed hurts. We get this one for free from the ascendancy.

      For damage, **Path Seeker** and **Traveller’s Wisdom** carry the build. **Path Seeker** gives us extra passive skill points, and **Traveller’s Wisdom** turns the small attribute nodes all over the tree into damage. On top of that we go full critical hits, and we have rage to multiply the damage.

      For defence we stack evasion, because **Sustainable Practices** turns evasion into elemental damage reduction. Evasion alone has a weakness: you evade, evade, evade, get hit, and die, because you have few ways of mitigating the hit. So we add deflection, we take part of our damage from mana before life, we have the elemental damage reduction, and the **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel** turns the part of a hit we cannot stop into damage we take over time.

  - title: "Skill Gems"
    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Pathfinder** changes.

      **Lunar Assault**. **Rakiata’s Flow** and **Rigwald’s Ferocity** are in the Full build, but you can play without them. Just know that if you remove **Rakiata’s Flow**, you have to put elemental penetration in your tree to make up the difference.

      **Berserk**. We use it because it improves our damage and our defences a lot, and you do need life regeneration to play it. That is why **Tecrod’s Gaze** is in the body armour, which regenerates 1.5% of our maximum life per second, and why **Vitality II** is a support on it. **Dialla’s Desire** is not needed. I have it because I have it, and it is good, but you can play without it.

      **Overwhelming Presence**. We use rare gloves instead of a unique, and this aura makes enemies easier to stun and to inflict elemental ailments on, so we freeze things more easily.

      **Wind Dancer**. This is where we get more evasion. It grants stages of a buff that gives more evasion per stage, and when we are hit it consumes the stages and damages the enemies around us. **Blind II**, **Maim**, and **Magnified Area II** apply to that hit, and **Life Leech III** keeps our life up.

      **Mana Remnants**. This is what fixes our mana. When we critically hit, we drop a mana remnant, and we crit a lot while clearing, so we are getting remnants all the time. Mana is zero issue with this build. **Harmonic Remnants II** and **Remnant Potency I** support it.

      **Eternal Rage**. The **Absent Amulet** grants it, so it costs no spirit. **Vitality I** and **Uhtred’s Omen** are its supports, and **Uhtred’s Omen** needs exactly one other support.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[pathfinder-full-endgame]

      The rare items below are described by what they give us, because the bases change for everyone depending on their resistances, attributes, and the rest of their gear. Besides the weapon and the jewellery, we want evasion on everything we wear.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Spiny Talisman**. It has a high base critical hit chance, and this build goes full critical hits. **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** and **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony** are socketed for extra damage.

      **Helmet**. A rare helmet with evasion. It has a **Raven-Touched Shard** in the socket, which lets me anoint it with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket. The rare jewel in that socket gives evasion as well.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Grace of the Ancestors** anointed. **Grace of the Ancestors** makes every rage also grant evasion rating, and it gives 10% increased attack speed.

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. A chest with more evasion would give more elemental damage reduction, but the opportunity cost is spirit and attributes, which we need. Maybe a rare chest with a lot of evasion that also has spirit and attributes is possible. **Morior Invictus** gives plus 7 to all attributes for every filled socket, and it helps with resistances across the board. Four sockets is nothing to scoff at, and I use them for **Tecrod’s Gaze**, which pays for **Berserk**.

      **Belt**. **Ingenuity**. It increases the bonuses from both of our rings, and with them the attributes.

      **Rings**. One of them is an **Unset Ring** with all attributes and strength, for the extra skill slot. Ingenuity amplifies the attributes from both rings, and the wolf needs a lot of attributes.

      **Gloves**. Rare evasion gloves. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and the **Emerald** in mine gives evasion.

      **Boots**. Rare evasion boots.

      **Attributes**. The wolf is starved for attributes. It wants intelligence and strength, and since we are evasion based, we also need dexterity to equip the evasion pieces. Because **Traveller’s Wisdom** spends the small attribute nodes on damage, the attributes come from the gear. **Morior Invictus** and the **Unset Ring** under **Ingenuity** give most of them, and the notables in the tree give the rest.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      The ascendancy comes first. **Relentless Pursuit** is the first one, and on the way there we get skill speed. Then we get one passive skill point, and then **Path Seeker**. It lets us allocate passive skills from the starting point of the Warrior or the Sorceress, and above all it grants 4 passive skill points. We pick **Path of the Warrior** for those points, because we do not use the Warrior or the Sorceress side of the tree. After that we get another passive skill point, then **Traveller’s Wisdom**, and **Sustainable Practices** after it. In total the ascendancy gives us 6 extra passive skill points, and an evasion node on the way.

      **Traveller’s Wisdom** is the damage. Normally the small nodes that grant strength, intelligence, or dexterity can instead be made to grant increased damage, armour, evasion, and energy shield, or cost efficiency. Every one of them in my tree grants 5% increased damage. I fix my mana and my defences elsewhere, and the amount of damage that gives us is insane. If you feel you are not tanky enough, you can come in and put increased armour, evasion, and energy shield on them, and if you need more mana, you can put cost efficiency. I have damage on every one of them.

      **Sustainable Practices** is the defence. 50% of our evasion rating also grants elemental damage reduction, and it does not depend on evading. We have the elemental damage reduction across the board just by stacking evasion, so we stack evasion. When you play the **Pathfinder** you want to go evasion because of it, unless you could pick something else, and the other options in the ascendancy are bad for a werewolf. One is about not being heavily stunned while sprinting and moving slower while using skills, and we stand in place when we attack. The others are poison, concoctions, and keeping life flask effects while life is full, and none of them matters here. That is why I picked **Sustainable Practices**, and I wanted it anyway.

      That is also why the tree goes down the evasion side and not the Warrior or Sorceress side. I did the Path of Building calculations for moving 20 points over to the Sorceress side and for starting from the Warrior side. Do not do that. Do not torture yourself. Go evasion and crit.

      The rest of the defence is in the tree. **Enhanced Reflexes**, **Enduring Deflection**, **Trained Deflection**, **Beastial Skin**, **Escape Velocity**, and **The Wild Cat** give evasion rating and deflection, and **Trained Deflection** and **Enduring Deflection** prevent a share of the damage from deflected hits. **Escape Strategy** and **Careful Consideration** are two sides of the same idea. One gives a large bonus to evasion rating if we have been hit recently and a penalty if we have not, and the other does the reverse. With both, one of them is always paying out and the other is costing less, so we have a net increase to evasion rating whether we have been hit or not. **Lucidity** makes 8% of our damage taken from mana before life, which is a lot of tankiness, and **Mana Remnants** keeps the mana there to pay for it.

      **Chaos Inoculation** is the only keystone in the radius of the **Undying Hate Timeless Jewel**, and the jewel turns it into **Sacrifice of Flesh**. When we take damage from hits, 20% of the life loss is prevented, and then we lose 150% of what was prevented over 4 seconds. So we do not take 100% of the hit at once, we take 80%, and the rest arrives slowly, which doesn’t matter because we have a lot of regeneration and a flask to press. It is basically a one-shot prevention mechanic, and it covers the hits that evasion and deflection let through.

      For attributes, **True Strike**, **Honed Instincts**, and **The Wild Cat** give plus 10 dexterity, **For the Jugular** gives plus 10 intelligence, and **Lucidity** gives plus 15 intelligence.

      The critical hit side of the tree is the rest of the damage. **Struck Through** gives plus 1% to critical hit chance for attacks, **True Strike**, **Serrated Edges**, **Heartstopping**, and **Careful Assassin** give critical hit chance, and **Throatseeker**, **Cooked**, **Heartbreaking**, **For the Jugular**, and **Desensitisation** give critical damage bonus. **Throatseeker** reduces our critical hit chance, **Cooked** reduces our armour, evasion, and energy shield, and **Careful Assassin** reduces our critical damage bonus, and the large bonuses they give pay for that. Rage comes with it, and **Berserk** strengthens it. The **Time-Lost Emerald** makes notables also grant critical hit chance and critical damage bonus, and small passives also grant evasion rating and attack damage. **Heart of the Well** gives extra fire and chaos damage, critical hit chance, critical damage bonus, and life regeneration, and the other **Emerald**s carry evasion, attack damage, critical hit chance, and critical damage bonus.

      The rest is a few damage notables. **Chakra of Impact** gives attack damage and more damage for each combo consumed, which suits **Cross Slash**. **Maiming Strike** gives attack damage and a chance to maim, **Stupefy** and **Dizzying Hits** daze and punish dazed enemies, **Thin Ice** and **Heavy Frost** reward the frozen enemies we make, **Catalysis** gives elemental damage with attacks, **Coming Calamity** gives cold damage while **Herald of Ice** is active, and **Honed Instincts** gives attack speed.

      **From Nothing** lets us allocate the nodes in the radius of **Pain Attunement** without a connected path. Four notables are in range. **Desensitisation** gives critical damage bonus and reduces the critical damage bonus of hits against us, **Hide of the Bear** gives armour while shapeshifted, **Bringer of Order** gives increased damage, and **Pack Encouragement** gives attack damage for each minion in our presence, which the wolves from **Pounce** provide. **Brutus’ Brain** makes them unable to take damage, so they cannot die.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage** and **The Noble Wolf**. **Primal Rage** gives plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, which means more evasion from **Grace of the Ancestors** and more damage from **Berserk**. **The Noble Wolf** gives critical hit chance and increased ailment magnitude against marked enemies. The jewel’s third enchant is a spell notable that does nothing for a werewolf.
