      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Blood Mage**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The rotation is the one from the general guide, and nothing changes in it. The first thing I look at on any new ascendancy is what it gives the werewolf, and the **Blood Mage** gives tankiness, which is where the werewolf struggles the most. Damage is easy to get, tankiness is not. **Crimson Power** and **Grasping Wounds** are the two best things in the ascendancy, and I take them without thinking about it. **Sanguimancy** comes for free and adds life remnants on top.

      I hoped the ascendancy would make the werewolf a bleeder. **Blood Barbs** makes elemental damage contribute to bleeding magnitude, and the werewolf deals mostly elemental damage, so it looked like the perfect fit. Every version of full bleeding I tried came out weaker than the build in this guide, including splits into the Mercenary and Ranger sides of the tree, because reliable bleeding needs support gems, chance to bleed, and bleeding magnitude on your gear. Bleeding stays a small bonus, and the build is a classic stack of energy shield, spell damage, and rage.

      What makes it different from the other werewolves is **Whispers of the Flesh** and how it works with curses. **Blasphemy** curses everything we enter combat with, cursed enemies start the fight with part of their life reserved, and **Enfeeble** makes them hit us for less. That tankiness is strong enough that it makes me rethink my older builds. **Enfeeble** in a **Blasphemy** could make them tankier, and the points and gear we save on defence can go into damage, like using **Ingenuity** instead of **Shavronne’s Satchel**.

      Damage comes from two places. **Crown of Eyes** makes spell damage apply to attacks, so we stack spell damage, and **Mystical Rage** gives spell damage for every rage we have. The other source is critical hits, from a **Spiny Talisman**, the tree, and the jewels.

  - title: "Skill Gems"
    content: |
      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Blood Mage** changes.

      **Blasphemy** and **Enfeeble**. This pair holds the build together. **Blasphemy** turns the curse into an aura, so every enemy that enters combat with us is already cursed and there is no delay. **Enfeeble** makes cursed enemies deal less damage, which is another layer of tankiness, **Heightened Curse** makes it stronger, and **Magnified Area II** widens it. We want cursed targets for two reasons. **Whispers of the Flesh** only works on cursed targets, and the bleeding from **Blood Barbs** is aggravated on them.

      **Life Remnants**. **Sanguimancy** gives us this skill, and it drops life remnants as we hit and kill enemies. Picking one up heals us, so it is free damage reduction on top of **Grasping Wounds**. It is supported by **Khatal’s Rejuvenation**, which gives us cooldown recovery whenever we pick up a remnant. **Arctic Howl**, **Cross Slash**, **Lunar Blessing**, and **Pounce** all have cooldowns, so they come back faster. **Remnant Potency I** and **Harmonic Remnants II** are the other supports.

      **Mana Remnants**. This is what fixes our mana. It drops remnants from critical hits against enemies with an elemental ailment, and we critically hit frozen enemies all the time, so we are always picking them up. The supports make us collect them without paying attention, and this build has no mana problems. **Harmonic Remnants II** and **Remnant Potency I** support it.

      **Berserk**. It strengthens our rage, and rage is a big part of our damage. **Vitality I** gives the life regeneration that pays for it, and **Uhtred’s Augury** needs exactly two other supports, so **Dialla’s Desire** is the second one.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[blood-mage-full-endgame]

      The rare items below are described by what they give us, because the bases change for everyone depending on their resistances, attributes, and the rest of their gear. Almost everything we wear wants energy shield.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Spiny Talisman** with increased critical hit chance, because this build goes full critical hits. **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** and **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony** are socketed for extra damage.

      **Helmet**. **Crown of Eyes**. Increases and reductions to spell damage also apply to attacks, so all the spell damage we stack becomes damage for the werewolf. Its **Raven-Touched Shard** lets me anoint it with **Battle Trance** for 8 more maximum rage, and **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** adds 4 more.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** that grants **Eternal Rage** at no spirit cost. It is anointed with **Zarokh’s Gift** for an extra jewel socket.

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. The energy shield on our body armour is what **Crimson Power** turns into maximum life, so this chest feeds the ascendancy as well. Every filled socket gives life regeneration, elemental resistances, and spirit, and **Tecrod’s Gaze** in a socket regenerates 1.5% of our maximum life per second, which pays for **Berserk**.

      **Gloves**. Rare energy shield gloves with flat damage to attacks. The **Cadigan’s Epiphany** rune destroys the augment sockets and turns them into a jewel socket, and mine holds a rare **Sapphire** with spell damage.

      **Boots**. Rare energy shield boots.

      **Rings**. Two rare rings with flat damage to attacks, and one of them is an **Unset Ring** for the extra skill slot. Flat damage on the gloves and rings is a lot of damage for a werewolf, and an **Ingenuity** belt boosts the rings even more.

      **Belt**. **Shavronne’s Satchel**. This is the item that fixes the one real problem of the build. We are full energy shield and have no evasion, so enemies keep hitting us, and energy shield does not recharge while we are being hit. **Shavronne’s Satchel** makes life recovery from flasks also apply to energy shield, so drinking the life flask recovers our energy shield too. It has to be a life flask with instant recovery. A normal flask stops working when life is full, so the extra would never reach the energy shield, and instant recovery is the only way to apply it in one go. If you do not use **Shavronne’s Satchel**, this does not matter.

      You might not need the belt. If you have so much damage that enemies die before they hit you, the energy shield recovers on its own, and then **Ingenuity**, **Headhunter**, or whatever else you have gives more damage. If you struggle to recover your energy shield, use **Shavronne’s Satchel**.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      ::tree-image[blood-mage]

      The ascendancy comes first. **Crimson Power** gives additional maximum life equal to 100% of the energy shield on our body armour, and the small maximum life nodes on the way come with it. **Ancient Aegis** on the tree raises the energy shield of that armour, so it feeds **Crimson Power** too.

      **Grasping Wounds** prevents 25% of the life lost from hits, and that much life is lost over 4 seconds instead. We are negating 25% of the damage all the time, because the damage that arrives over those four seconds is damage we regenerate. We recover it with regeneration, with the flask, and with the life remnants from **Sanguimancy**, which every **Blood Mage** gets for free.

      **Whispers of the Flesh** is the damage. Targets cursed by us have 100% reduced life regeneration rate and at least 15% of their life reserved. Every time we enter combat with something we remove the top 15% of its life, and that is damage. It needs cursed targets, which is what **Blasphemy** and **Enfeeble** are for.

      **Bleed on Critical Chance** gives a 15% chance to inflict bleeding on a critical hit, and since we critically hit all the time we are bleeding all the time. **Blood Barbs** makes elemental damage contribute to bleeding magnitude, and bleeding on cursed targets is aggravated. Together they are about a 10% damage increase, which is modest and still better than the other options, so we take them.

      The rest of the ascendancy is skipped. The base critical hit chance and critical hit chance for spells are useless because we use no spells, and so is **Vitality Siphon**, which leeches spell damage as life. **Gore Spike** gives critical damage bonus per 50 current life and is good, but it costs too many points to reach. **Sanguine Tides** could give a lot of damage, because we are always spending life and it turns life spent into flask charges that grant physical damage. It also stops flasks from recovering life, and we need the flask.

      For damage, we go to the spell side of the tree because of **Crown of Eyes**. **Potent Incantation** gives 30% increased spell damage, and the jewel sockets on the way hold rare **Sapphire**s with spell damage, critical hit chance, critical damage bonus, and maximum energy shield.

      What makes it insane is **From Nothing**. It lets us allocate the passives in the radius of **Pain Attunement** without a connected path, and that gets us **Mystical Rage**: every rage also grants 2% increased spell damage. Rage normally gives damage to attacks and nothing to spells. Now it gives both, and **Crown of Eyes** turns the spell damage into attack damage, so we double dip on rage. **Eternal Rage** generates it, **Berserk** strengthens it, and **Battle Trance** and my **Megalomaniac** raise the maximum. The **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage**, which gives plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, and **Crushing Verdict**, which gives 50% increased attack damage and 30% increased stun buildup and reduces attack speed by 5%.

      The second source of damage is critical hits. **Struck Through** gives plus 1% critical hit chance for attacks, **Heartstopping**, **Critical Exploit**, and **Careful Assassin** give critical hit chance, and **Spirit of the Wolf** gives critical hit chance and skill speed while shapeshifted. **Throatseeker**, **Heartbreaking**, and **Desensitisation** give critical damage bonus. **Throatseeker** reduces our critical hit chance and **Careful Assassin** reduces our critical damage bonus, and the large bonuses they give pay for that. So we hit hard, and we always have rage.

      The rest is the energy shield pool. **Pure Energy**, **Patient Barrier**, **Enhanced Barrier**, **Melding**, **Insightfulness**, and **Dampening Shield** give maximum energy shield, and **Enhanced Barrier** also converts part of our maximum life to energy shield. My build ends up with about 2,000 life and 6,000 energy shield, roughly 8,500 in total, and you can push it higher if you want to focus on it. Recovering that energy shield is the real difficulty, and the belt in the gear section covers it.
