      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Gemling Legionnaire**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Gemling Legionnaire** gets a lot of power from gem quality, and that is what this build is about. The quality bonuses give us damage and tankiness. This is a tanky, comfortable build, and it is not the fastest one. I would rather not die than be fast, because dying costs you experience, loot, the map, and time. I can clear a 200% delirious map without worrying about anything killing me. Getting there takes investment, and everything in the build can still be upgraded.

      In my hideout I have 77% armour, 60% evasion, 23% block, and 61% deflection, and they all go higher in maps thanks to **Constricting Command**. The Late Endgame version covered here uses expensive pieces: **Rakiata’s Flow**, **Rigwald’s Ferocity**, **Her Declaration**, and **Seraph’s Heart**. You don’t need any of them.

      I clear faster when I keep my buffs up. Use **Savage Fury** every time it is ready, and keep moving instead of standing around picking up loot.

    content: |
      The [gem setups from the general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups) apply, so this section only covers what the **Gemling Legionnaire** changes.

      **Lunar Assault**. I use **Bhatair’s Vengeance** in place of **Ice Bite II**. **Ice Bite II** is better in 90% of scenarios, but when you stack a lot of rage like we do, **Bhatair’s Vengeance** is better. **Rigwald’s Ferocity** makes a skill hit harder in the second weapon set, so all my skills sit in the second weapon set. **Savage Fury** takes the first one, and I cover that in the spirit gems below.

      **Pounce**. **Pounce** runs **Brutus’ Brain** instead of **Freezing Mark**, because the wolves are what the **Gemling Legionnaire** gets out of **Pounce**. **Advanced Thaumaturgy** makes gem quality give **Pounce** more wolves, and I have 13 of them. **Predator’s Mark** makes the target take increased damage for each enemy within six meters of it, up to 45%, so 13 wolves give me 39% increased damage just by standing there. **Brutus’ Brain** makes the wolves deal zero damage, and it also makes them immortal. They are there to make me deal more damage. **Mark of Siphoning** replaces **Mark for Death II**, so I leech mana from the target. The wolves still apply **Culling Strike II** with their hits even though they deal zero damage. With 13 wolves hitting, any target in **Culling Strike** range gets culled, so it is always active.

      **Arctic Howl**. In place of the damage supports, I use **Cooldown Recovery II** so I can howl more often, and **Deep Freeze** so a frozen target stays frozen longer.

      **Cross Slash**. **Close Combat II** takes the place of **Elemental Armament II** here.

      **Lunar Blessing**. **Lunar Blessing** turns you and your wolves into white wolves. **Uhtred’s Constellation** replaces **Cooldown Recovery II**. It makes **Lunar Blessing** deal 8% more damage for each different skill with a cooldown you used in the last 8 seconds, and it doesn’t matter whether you used them before or after **Lunar Blessing**. **Arctic Howl**, **Pounce**, and **Cross Slash** are three cooldowns, so the moonbeams deal 24% more damage. It also gives **Lunar Blessing** two extra cooldown uses. The downside is 50% less cooldown recovery rate, but the **Gemling Legionnaire** gives **Lunar Blessing** increased cooldown recovery rate, which covers it. If you aren’t going to use **Uhtred’s Constellation**, don’t use **Second Wind** either. Use **Cooldown Recovery II** instead. **Oisín’s Oath** takes the place of **Atalui’s Bloodletting**. It makes mana leech recover based on elemental damage instead of physical damage. The werewolf is hungry for mana, and we deal elemental damage, so this keeps us leeching mana all the time. It is cheap.

      **Berserk**. This is the most important skill in the build. I double corrupted it to level 21 with 23 quality, because we want all the quality and levels we can get. At that level, **Berserk** gives 63% increased rage effect and 16 maximum rage. Its quality adds another 20% increased rage effect, for 83% in total, which almost doubles everything rage gives us. I’m at 86 maximum rage. **Dialla’s Desire** adds a level and quality to **Berserk** and reduces its spirit reservation. **Uhtred’s Omen** adds two levels as long as exactly one other support modifies the skill, so **Dialla’s Desire** is the only other support on **Berserk**. **Dialla’s Desire** and **Uhtred’s Omen** are expensive, and I got them last. You don’t need them.

      **Berserk** makes you lose 0.1% of your maximum life per rage per second, so at 86 rage you lose a lot of life, and **Eternal Rage** keeps you at full rage all the time. I don’t like my life going down, even when I’m AFK in my hideout, so I want enough life regeneration to cover it. Multiply your rage by 0.001, then multiply that by your maximum life. I have 86 rage and 2,362 life, so I need about 203 life regeneration to break even. You can sit a little under that, because you also get life from kills, leech, and regeneration while surrounded. This is why **Vitality** is in the build. I also run **Clarity** for mana sustain. I could take out **Clarity** and put in a second **Vitality**, and I would be over the cap in regeneration. Check your own rage and life, do the math, and decide what you can afford. **Morior Invictus** can also give life regeneration per socket filled, which is another source.

      **Eternal Rage**. I use an **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**, which gives me **Eternal Rage** for free and frees up 100 spirit. I spend part of that on **Her Declaration** and **Seraph’s Heart**. **Her Declaration** intimidates enemies in your presence, so I’m tankier and hit harder. **Seraph’s Heart** makes enemy hits have a 20% chance to treat all your resistances as 90%, and it costs 45 spirit. Both are expensive and neither is required. If you would rather keep a normal amulet with six modifiers, free up 100 spirit by cutting something else, like **Clarity**, and run **Eternal Rage** as a regular spirit gem.

      **Other spirit gems**. **Wind Dancer** is my defensive layer, since I’m a hybrid of armour and evasion. **Savage Fury** sits in the first weapon set, because the second one is where my skills need to be for **Rigwald’s Ferocity**. It still builds up but doesn’t take your spirit away. You use it, and when you swap back to the other weapon set you keep the buff without paying the spirit.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[gemling-late-endgame]

      **Weapon**. An **Alpha Talisman**, for the block chance. It has an implicit with block chance, and I bought one with 18%. You don’t have to use it. If you don’t, put the block points you take out of the tree into other defenses, like armour, deflection, or evasion. I like block a lot, and when I checked my effective health pool in Path of Building, it was night and day with block. I don’t use a **Maji Talisman** for more rage, because there is such a thing as too much rage. Over 100 rage it becomes a pain to cap unless you can fill it instantly, and 86 rage is plenty. The rest of the weapon is physical damage, strength, attack speed, and levels of melee skills. In the sockets I use **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky**, which gives 5% of damage as extra damage of all elements, and **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony**. **Fenumus’ Rune of Agony** is weird, but it gave me more damage than any other rune I tested.

      **Helmet**. **Constricting Command**, for the surrounded tech, which is overpowered. Some people think it was nerfed because **Constricting Command** can no longer reduce the enemies needed by five, but that only affects ranged builds. We are melee, so we are always in range of an enemy. **Constricting Command** reduces the requirement by up to four, so one enemy keeps us surrounded all the time and we always get the bonuses. It also gives life regeneration per second, all attributes, and maximum life.

      A **Raven-Touched Shard** in the helmet lets me instil a notable passive, and I instil **Vengeful Fury**. Normally rage gives 1% more attack damage per rage, so 86 rage gives 86% more damage, and **Berserk**’s rage effect pushes it over 100%. **Vengeful Fury** adds 1% increased armour per rage, and **Berserk** boosts that too, so I get over 100% increased armour from rage. The other socket holds **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity**, which gives plus 4 maximum rage. If your **Constricting Command** has only one socket, use the soul core.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**. I anoint it with **Grace of the Ancestors**, which gives 10% increased attack speed and makes every rage grant 1% increased evasion rating. At 86 rage, **Berserk** pushes that over 100% increased evasion. So rage gives me damage, armour, and evasion. **Grace of the Ancestors** is a hidden anoint that isn’t connected to the tree, and you can only get it through an anoint from something like a **Megalomaniac**. It was introduced in 0.5. My amulet rolls strength to balance my attributes, increased evasion rating, levels of melee skills, and increased global armour, evasion and energy shield.

      **Body armour**. **Morior Invictus**. I recommend at least spirit and all elemental resistances per socket filled. The third modifier is flexible. Mine is maximum mana per socket filled, because I was running out of mana. You can take life regeneration per socket filled instead, which is very comfy with **Berserk**. It doesn’t have to be a five socket one, and a four socket one works. My sockets hold three copies of **Soul Core of Tacati** for chaos resistance, **The Greatwolf’s Rune of Willpower**, which makes 15% of damage be taken from mana before life, and **Tecrod’s Gaze**, which regenerates 1.5% of maximum life per second and is very cheap.

      **Belt**. **Darkness Enthroned**. It increases the effect of socketed augment items, and it counts items socketed in it as if it were body armour. That means anything I socket in it gets the body armour bonuses, boosted. It has two sockets, and I use two tricks.

      First, **Fox Idol**. Its bonded modifier is plus quality to all skills, and in the belt it is boosted to 9% instead of the 5% you get in a chest. We want all the quality we can get. If you don’t have **Darkness Enthroned**, you can put the **Fox Idol** in your chest.

      Second, the **Rune of the Blossom**. People use it while leveling. In body armour it gives 50 spirit, but you lose 1 spirit per 2 levels, so at endgame it is useless. In **Darkness Enthroned**, that spirit is boosted, and I get 99 spirit from it. After the spirit I lose from my levels, I’m still left with around 40 extra spirit. For this to work, your **Darkness Enthroned** must not reach 100% increased effect. Mine is 98%, and 99% is perfect. At 100%, the level penalty doubles to minus 2 spirit per 2 levels and cancels the gain.

      **Gloves**. **Sine Aequo**, and I think they are non-negotiable. You immobilise enemies at 50% buildup instead of 100%, which is overpowered for freezing everything. We want to freeze everything as fast as possible, because **Shred** destroys frozen enemies and we get many benefits from frozen enemies. With **Sine Aequo**, you don’t have to think about freeze buildup. **Cadigan’s Epiphany** gave my gloves a jewel socket. In it I have life regeneration, melee damage, maximum rage, and increased effect of prefixes.

      **Boots**. Normal armour and evasion boots. I roll deflection, life regeneration, fire resistance, armour, and movement speed.

      **Rings**. Flat damage, mana per enemy killed, elemental resistance, and attributes. Attributes matter a lot. You need at least one source of mana leech, and it can be on the weapon or either ring. The rest is life, resistance, and damage.

      **Flasks and charms**. I use **Lavianga’s Spirits**, which is very good for mana and always gives me a flask effect for the tree. I also run a good life flask. For chaos resistance, I use the **Forsaken Bangle**, an Amethyst Charm. When a chaos hit lands, it raises your chaos resistance to your cap. If you don’t have one, use a normal Amethyst Charm.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      ::tree-image[gemling-legionnaire]

      The **Gemling Legionnaire** ascendancy is the whole reason to play this build. **Advanced Thaumaturgy** makes gem quality grant an additional effect, which is what powers the **Pounce** wolves and **Berserk**. **Gem Studded** gives a bonus based on your most numerous support gem color. My most numerous is red, which gives me hits against you have no critical damage bonus, so I take it for tankiness. **Integrated Efficiency** gives 20% increased damage per connected red support gem, 6% increased skill speed per connected green support gem, and 20% increased critical hit chance per connected blue support gem. We don’t scale crits in this build, so the blue part matters least.

      I take **Primal Rage** and **Vengeful Fury** as the two most important picks outside the ascendancy. **Primal Rage** gives plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, and it is fundamental to the build. You need a way to get it, whether that is a **Megalomaniac**, an anoint on your amulet, or something else. My **Megalomaniac** gives me **Primal Rage**. It would be great to get **Primal Rage** and **Vengeful Fury** on the same **Megalomaniac**, but that is very expensive, so I didn’t. **Vengeful Fury** is on my helmet, as described in the gear section. On top of those, **Battle Trance** gives plus 8 maximum rage, and **Ignore Pain** gives rage when I get hit and makes every rage grant increased stun threshold.

      Block scales our damage. **Lay Siege** gives 1% increased damage per 1% block chance, so the more block you take, the more damage you deal, and that is why I use the **Alpha Talisman**. **Favourable Odds** gives more block chance while surrounded, which also feeds **Lay Siege**. If you want more block, you can take a few more block points in the tree. I took some of them out because I needed the points.

      **Constricting Command** keeps us surrounded, so the surrounded nodes are worth a lot. **Frantic Fighter** gives a big boost to attack damage while surrounded, **Thrill of Battle** gives attack speed, **Tactical Retreat** gives movement speed, and **In the Thick of It** gives life regeneration. **Presence Present** gives 35% increased attack damage when you have an ally in your presence. My wolves are immortal, so I always have one.

      For defenses, **General’s Bindings** gives 8% of evasion rating as extra armour, and **Leather Bound Gauntlets** gives evasion rating equal to the armour on my gloves. **Sine Aequo** has 192 armour, so I get 192 base evasion from it. That is base evasion, so it gets multiplied by all my other bonuses. **Backup Plan** gives evasion if I was hit recently and armour if I wasn’t. **Path of the Renegade** makes 20% of armour also apply to chaos damage, and **Pure Chaos** gives 11% of damage as extra chaos damage.

      I run 17 red, 12 blue, and 10 green support gems. That is enough for **Gem Enthusiast**, which gives 5% increased maximum life with at least 10 red support gems, 5% increased maximum mana with at least 10 blue, and 5% increased movement speed with at least 10 green. I get all three.

      I socket **From Nothing** at **Chaos Inoculation**, which lets me reach **Heavy Frost**. Mine is double corrupted with chaos resistance, which isn’t needed. The part that matters is that passives in radius of **Chaos Inoculation** can be allocated without being connected to your tree.

      I also use a **Time-Lost Ruby** in the tree. One of its modifiers gives the notables in its radius extra block chance, and another gives them plus 1 maximum rage each. That adds up to several extra maximum rage across the notables I have there.

      The rest of the tree stacks armour, evasion, deflection, and block. The surrounded tech makes us much tankier in maps than in my hideout. In maps I regenerate life while surrounded, which cancels out **Berserk**’s life loss, and I go into the positive. If you want to be more comfortable, you can put more regeneration in the tree.
