      This guide builds on the [general werewolf guide](/werewolf-general/) and only covers what is specific to the **Spirit Walker**. If a skill, support, or mechanic here is unclear, read the general guide first.

      The **Spirit Walker** is a huntress ascendancy built around spirit companions: a bear from **Wild Protector**, stags from **Vivid Stampede**, and an owl from **Primal Bounty**. We keep playing the same werewolf rotation, and the spirits fight next to us. That makes it the busiest werewolf on screen, and it is a lot of fun to play.

      The first thing to fix on any werewolf is defence. None of the werewolf skills have a defensive component, and the werewolf wants to be in the middle of the fight, so we are always in a bad position. The huntress sits on the evasion side of the tree, so we go evasion, or evasion with energy shield. Evasion can never reach 100%, so a hit will eventually land, and if that hit kills us, nothing else matters. What we need is a big maximum hit. Deflection reduces the damage of the hits we fail to evade, but a large enough hit still kills us after the reduction. The ascendancy gives us a way to push that further, which is to make our companions take the damage instead of us.

      It works like this. When **Wild Protector**, **Primal Bounty**, and **Vivid Stampede** are all allocated, **Sacred Unity** gives the bear **Embrace of the Wild**. Its allies regenerate 2% of maximum life per second while they are in its presence, and 8% of the damage they would take is taken by the bear. That alone is a real maximum hit gain. From there we collect every other source of damage redirection we can find. **Forgotten Warden** takes part of every deflected hit from a companion’s life, gives companions more maximum life, and grants **Spirit Vessel**, which is a companion of its own. **Starkonja’s Head**, **Captivating Companionship**, and **Loyalty** each take another share of every hit. Companions need to survive for this to work, because once one dies it stops absorbing damage, which is why so much of the build also scales companion life.

      The damage side is a werewolf build and not a minion build. If I wanted to play minions, I would play a minion ascendancy. **Inspiring Ally** is what makes that possible: increases and reductions to companion damage also apply to us, so every companion damage node on the tree works twice. On top of that we get **Pack Encouragement** for the minions around us, and a large amount of critical hit chance and critical damage bonus from the weapon and the tree.

      The setups follow the [general guide](/werewolf-general/#gem-setups). Everything below is what the **Spirit Walker** changes.

      **Wild Protector**. This comes from the ascendancy, and it is the bear. It does not count toward our companion limit. The bear is there to soak damage, so its supports make it big and hard to kill. **Hulking Minions** makes it gigantic with more life. That support costs extra spirit, but **Wild Protector** reserves none, so for this skill we get the life for free. **Romira’s Requital** makes the bear take 10% of the hits we would take, and we recoup 200% of that damage as life. **Meat Shield II** makes it take 40% less damage, and its drawback of less damage dealt does not matter on a bear that is only there to tank. **Elemental Army** adds 30% to all its elemental resistances, and **Catha’s Brilliance** blinds and ignites enemies near it.

      **Spirit Vessel**. This comes from the **Forgotten Warden**. It summons a shapeshifting companion that uses copies of the shapeshift skills socketed in it, and I socket **Lunar Blessing**. The vessel transforms into a werewolf and rains moonbeams on the enemies, and since it is a companion with life, we can redirect damage to it. **Loyalty** makes it take 10% of the hits we would take, at the cost of 30% less maximum life, and **Hulking Minions** takes that life back and more. **Tecrod’s Revenge** keeps it fighting for up to 20 seconds after its life reaches 0, so it keeps casting **Lunar Blessing** after a big hit instead of dropping out. The game may tell you that you cannot have more companions. That is normal, and the vessel is still there. We still use our own **Lunar Blessing** exactly as in the general guide.

      **Vivid Stampede**. This also comes from the ascendancy. Every 10 metres we move gives us a vivid wisp, up to 3, and when we attack the wisps are spent to send a stag leaping forward per wisp. Each stag releases a shockwave every time it lands. We attack all the time, so it triggers all the time, and the stags kill a lot of things before we get close to them. **Overcharge** gives more shock magnitude, and it reflects the shock back at us. **Commiserate** removes the ailment from us every time the supported skill inflicts one, and it makes ailments stronger if we are afflicted when we inflict them. We shock ourselves and cleanse ourselves in the same breath, and since a stag hits many times while leaping, the later hits get the stronger shock. A skill that hits only once would not work with this. **Shock** adds chance to shock, and **Magnified Area II** and **Elemental Armament II** do what they do everywhere else.

      **Primal Bounty**. This is the owl, and we take it only to unlock **Sacred Unity**. It empowers the next projectile skill we use, and we use none. Even a projectile skill triggered by something else does not get the empowerment. We do not put any gem setup on it.

      **Eternal Rage**. The **Absent Amulet** carries it, so it costs no spirit. It costs 100 spirit otherwise, and the amulet frees that spirit for the rest of the build.

      **Overwhelming Presence**. I use rare gloves instead of **Sine Aequo**, so we lose the immobilise at 50% buildup. **Overwhelming Presence** makes enemies in our presence easier to inflict elemental ailments on, so they freeze much faster, and that makes up for it. It costs spirit, which is why **Eternal Rage** sits on the amulet. **Uhtred’s Omen** needs exactly one other support, and **Armour Demolisher II** is that support here.

      **Wind Dancer and Savage Fury**. **Wind Dancer** is the evasion defensive layer from the general guide, and I run it together with **Savage Fury**, which keeps us in onslaught. To fit both I wear an **Unset Ring**, which gives one more skill slot, and the Gear section explains it. If you do not want it, drop **Savage Fury** and keep **Wind Dancer**. With enough spirit left over you can use that room for a second companion with **Loyalty**, and it adds damage, but it is a pain to set up and costs you things elsewhere. This setup is already enough for the companion part of the build.

  - title: "Gear"
    content: |
      ::build-gear[spirit-walker-full-endgame]

      The bases of the rare items below are the ones I wear, and they are not fixed. Yours will change depending on your resistances, attributes, and the rest of your gear. The one exception is the **Unset Ring**, which gives us the extra skill slot.

      **Weapon**. A rare **Spiny Talisman**. I picked it for its 5% base critical hit chance, because we build around critical hits. I want levels of melee skills first, then physical damage, fire damage, and critical hit chance. I socket **Saqawal’s Rune of the Sky** and two **Perfect Iron Rune**.

      **Helmet**. **Starkonja’s Head**. It makes 15% of the damage from hits come out of our companion’s life before it reaches us, and it also gives a lot of evasion, critical hit chance, and dexterity. I have the **Tzamoto’s Soul Core of Ferocity** for plus 4 maximum rage. **Grace of the Ancestors** is anointed on it, which I can do because the helmet has a **Raven-Touched Shard**. It gives attack speed, and every rage also grants evasion. If you do not have the shard, do not anoint it.

      **Amulet**. An **Absent Amulet** with **Eternal Rage**. I want evasion, levels of melee skills, resistances, and spirit. **Zarokh’s Gift** is anointed on it for an extra jewel socket.

      **Body armour**. **Forgotten Warden**. It is the centre of the build. Companions get 50% more maximum life, deflected hits take part of their damage from a companion’s life, and it grants **Spirit Vessel**. It also gives deflection rating for every 50 energy shield we are missing, and the base is evasion and energy shield, which is why the build wants both. I socket two **Perfect Iron Rune** and an **Ulaman’s Gaze**, which prevents 3% of the damage from deflected hits.

      **Belt**. A rare **Plate Belt**, which gives three charm slots. Besides the slots I want life, mana, strength, and resistances.

      **Rings**. Rare rings. One of them has to be an **Unset Ring**, because I need the extra skill slot to run **Wind Dancer** and **Savage Fury** together. You can use two if you want. We want flat damage to attacks and resistances, and mine are an **Unset Ring** and a **Prismatic Ring**. The ring bases will change for you based on your resistance needs, except for the **Unset Ring**.

      **Gloves**. Rare evasion and energy shield gloves instead of **Sine Aequo**, because we get more damage from them. I want flat damage to attacks, levels of melee skills, resistances, and evasion and energy shield. Mine are a **Secured Wraps** with **Cadigan’s Epiphany**, which turns the augment sockets into a jewel socket. In that socket is a rare **Emerald** with critical hit chance for attacks, attack speed, elemental damage, critical damage bonus for attacks, and the effect of suffixes.

      **Boots**. Rare evasion and energy shield boots, which for me are **Charmed Shoes**. I want 30% movement speed, dexterity, fire and chaos resistance, and the mod that grants deflection rating equal to a share of our evasion rating. I have a **Greater Iron Rune** in the socket.

      **Charms**. **Nascent Hope**, a **Thawing Charm** so you never stay frozen, which also starts our energy shield recharge when it triggers. **Forsaken Bangle**, which triggers when we take chaos damage from a hit and recoups half of the chaos damage it prevents as life and mana. The third is **Rite of Passage** with the Spirit of the Bear, which raises our maximum life and lowers the damage we take, and both are maximum hit. It is cheap and not required for the build, but I recommend it for the extra tankiness.
  - title: "Passive Tree"
    content: |
      On the ascendancy I take **Wild Protector**, **Primal Bounty**, **Vivid Stampede**, **Sacred Unity**, **The Mórrigan’s Guidance**, and **Shared Companion Damage**. Think of **Primal Bounty** as clicking **Sacred Unity**, so you are not losing a point to something you do not want. The 12% increased critical hit chance node on the way is also good. **The Mórrigan’s Guidance** gives us a vivid wisp back when **Vivid Stampede** ends, so the stags keep coming, and it makes each stag deal 20% more damage and have 20% more shock magnitude per leap. More shock magnitude means every other source of damage we deal is bigger too, and the stags do a lot of damage. **Shared Companion Damage** gives companions 10% increased damage and gives us 10% increased damage while a companion is in our presence, and it is worth a point because of **Inspiring Ally**, which I explain below.

      I skip **The Natural Order** and **Idolatry**, since both make the build lean harder into minions. **Idolatry** also forces idols, and runes are better for a werewolf. I skip **The Catha’s Balance** as well. It gives companions added damage equal to 60% of our weapon damage, and it only pays off with a second companion, at which point you are building minions.

      The defence is on the tree as much as on the gear. **Captivating Companionship** takes 5% of the damage from hits from our companion’s life before us, and gives more armour, evasion, and energy shield while our companion is nearby. **Nourishing Ally** gives companions 20% more maximum life, and **Sturdy Ally** makes them gain our strength. **Trained Deflection** and **Enduring Deflection** prevent more of the damage from deflected hits, along with the small deflection nodes around them. **Spectral Ward** gives energy shield for the evasion rating of our body armour, which is a lot with the **Forgotten Warden**, and **Patient Barrier** gives a larger energy shield pool at the cost of a slower start of recharge. **Chakra of Life** adds a little life and life recovery.

      **Inspiring Ally** is where a good part of the damage comes from. The small nodes that give companion damage and damage while a companion is in our presence now give both to us. One of them gives 12% increased companion damage and 10% increased damage while a companion is in our presence, and with **Inspiring Ally** that is 22% increased damage for us, and the companions still get their 12%. I take every one of them I can reach.

      I socket **From Nothing** on **Pain Attunement**, which lets me allocate the nodes in its radius without a connected path. That gets me **Pack Encouragement**, which gives 5% increased attack damage for each minion in our presence, up to 80%. We have the bear, the vessel companion, the seven wolves from **Pounce** with **Brutus’ Brain**, and the minions from **Living Lightning** on **Arctic Howl**. It also gets me **Bringer of Order** for increased damage and **Desensitisation** for critical damage bonus, without the nodes around them that I do not want.

      We go critical. On top of the weapon, the tree has **Heartstopping** and **Struck Through** for critical hit chance, and **Heartbreaking** for critical damage bonus. **Throatseeker** and **Careful Assassin** each give up a little of what the other one gives, and together they come out as 30% increased critical hit chance and 40% increased critical damage bonus. **Cooked** gives 60% increased critical damage bonus at the cost of 25% of our armour, evasion, and energy shield. I take it in the Full Endgame build and not in the Budget one. **Stupefy** and **Dizzying Hits** daze enemies, and then give us damage and critical hit chance against them. **Catalysis** gives elemental damage with attacks, and **Honed Instincts** and **Acceleration** give attack speed and skill speed. For freeze, which **Sine Aequo** no longer handles, I take **Heavy Frost** and **Thin Ice** as on all my werewolf builds.

      My **Megalomaniac** allocates **Primal Rage** and **Overwhelming Strike**. **Primal Rage** is plus 12 maximum rage while shapeshifted, and **Overwhelming Strike** adds critical hit chance and critical damage bonus for attacks.

      The jewels are mostly for critical hits. The three rare emeralds in the tree carry evasion, critical hit chance, attack damage, and critical damage bonus. The **Time-Lost Emerald** makes small passives in its radius also grant 2% increased evasion and 2% increased elemental damage, and notables grant 7% critical hit chance and 9% critical damage bonus for attacks.
