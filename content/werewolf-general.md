## Core Mechanics, Skills, and Gameplay

The Werewolf is a fast, active playstyle built around combining several abilities to maximize damage and survivability. You have a strong combination of movement speed, attack speed, and burst damage, but getting the most out of the form requires you to use your skills in the right order.

This guide covers the mechanics shared by all Werewolf builds in Path of Exile 2, including the core skills, single-target rotation, supporting Spirit Gems, passive-tree priorities, defensive considerations, and leveling options. Individual ascendancy guides will cover the choices specific to each character.

## Understanding the Werewolf

Talismans allow you to transform into three different forms: Bear, Werewolf, and Wyvern. Each form provides its own passive benefit. The Werewolf's benefit is increased movement speed.

Equipping a talisman and selecting Werewolf form also grants access to Shred, your default attack. Shred is a natural ability, meaning it levels up automatically as your character levels. You cannot use Jeweller's Orbs to add support-gem slots to it.

Shred unlocks its third support-gem slot at level 40, its fourth at level 65, and its fifth at level 90.

### An Active, Combo-Oriented Playstyle

The Werewolf has several abilities that work together in a specific sequence. You don't need to use every ability constantly, but you should have your core skills socketed and assigned to hotkeys so you can use them when needed.

This creates a very active playstyle. You have a clear rotation for maximizing single-target damage, and you regularly use your abilities to improve both damage and survivability. Despite requiring several buttons, the rotation feels fluid because the skills naturally follow one another.

Movement is another major part of the Werewolf's strength. You have high movement speed and attack speed, allowing you to cover terrain quickly, reposition around enemies, and dodge incoming attacks. Learning to use that mobility effectively is essential to getting the most out of the form.

## Core Werewolf Skills

### ![Shred icon](/images/werewolf-skills/shred.webp) Shred

Shred is your basic attack and one of your primary damage abilities. It becomes particularly important against frozen enemies because hitting them with Shred causes ice fragments to drop onto the ground. These fragments detonate after a short delay, dealing additional damage.

Shred also has a combo sequence. The first three attacks are followed by a stronger, slower fourth attack. For the purposes of your rotation, these four attacks make up one complete Shred combo.

When fighting a frozen target, you want to fit as many complete Shred combos as possible into the Freeze duration before finishing with Cross Slash.

### ![Lunar Assault icon](/images/werewolf-skills/lunar-assault.webp) Lunar Assault

Lunar Assault is primarily used to build up Freeze on enemies. Its damage is relatively low compared with your main damage sequence, but its Freeze buildup makes it an essential part of your rotation.

You use Lunar Assault to prime a target for Freeze, then follow up with Arctic Howl to freeze it. Once the target is frozen, you switch to Shred to generate ice fragments.

### ![Pounce icon](/images/werewolf-skills/pounce.webp) Pounce

Pounce is a mobility skill that lets you leap across the battlefield. It is useful for covering ground quickly, reaching enemies, and navigating elevation changes when there is no direct path available.

It also applies Predator's Mark to the target you hit. The mark increases the damage the target takes based on the number of enemies around it. When the mark activates, it can summon a wolf, allowing you to build up a pack of wolves as you continue fighting.

Pounce is also an important part of your single-target rotation. By supporting it with Charged Mark and Eternal Mark, you can trigger the mark's effects while keeping the target marked for subsequent attacks.

### ![Arctic Howl icon](/images/werewolf-skills/arctic-howl.webp) Arctic Howl

Arctic Howl is a warcry that helps you freeze enemies and empowers your next few attacks.

An enemy becomes primed for Freeze when its Freeze buildup reaches 70%. You can recognize this state by the percentage displayed on its health bar or by the blue bubble floating above its head.

Using Arctic Howl on a target that is primed for Freeze automatically freezes it and grants empowered attacks. This is why you should build up Freeze with Lunar Assault before using Arctic Howl. You freeze the target quickly while also gaining the empowered attacks needed for your damage sequence.

You can also use the Deep Freeze support gem with Arctic Howl to extend the duration of Freeze, giving you more time to attack with Shred before the target unfreezes.

### ![Cross Slash icon](/images/werewolf-skills/cross-slash.webp) Cross Slash

Cross Slash is your primary finisher. It deals damage in front of you and has two important interactions with the rest of your kit.

First, Cross Slash activates the mark on a marked target, triggering its associated effects and producing an additional shockwave when both gouges hit. Second, it gathers the ice fragments generated by Shred and detonates them immediately at the location where the gouges cross.

This makes Cross Slash especially powerful against frozen enemies. You can accumulate ice fragments with Shred, then detonate them all at once instead of waiting for them to explode individually.

You should also support Cross Slash with Biting Frost. This support consumes Freeze on the target to increase the damage of the hit. Because Cross Slash already benefits from detonating your ice fragments, using it against a frozen enemy combines the fragment damage with the increased damage from consuming Freeze.

### ![Lunar Blessing icon](/images/werewolf-skills/lunar-blessing.webp) Lunar Blessing

Lunar Blessing is one of the most important abilities in the entire Werewolf kit. It transforms you into the white Werewolf, increases your cold damage, and causes moonbeams to rain down on enemies when you hit them with melee skills.

The moonbeams deal substantial additional damage, making Lunar Blessing a major source of your damage output. You should aim to keep it active for as long as possible.

Lunar Blessing consumes all your current Rage when activated. The more Rage you have when you activate it, the longer the buff lasts. This means that managing Rage and reducing the skill's downtime are important parts of maintaining your damage.

## The Single-Target Damage Rotation

The Werewolf's single-target rotation combines marking the target, applying Shock, freezing the enemy, generating ice fragments, and detonating everything with Cross Slash. Executing the full sequence correctly produces a large burst of damage.

Before starting, make sure Lunar Blessing is active. Ideally, you should already be transformed into the white Werewolf when you enter combat.

Pounce > CS > Lunar Assault > Arctic Howl > Shred > CS

### Step 1: Pounce and Apply Predator's Mark

Start by using Pounce on your target. This applies Predator's Mark, which increases the damage the target takes based on the number of enemies around it.

Pounce should have both Charged Mark and Eternal Mark socketed as supports. Charged Mark creates Shocked Ground when the mark activates, while Eternal Mark prevents the mark from being consumed the first time it activates.

### Step 2: Cross Slash to Trigger the Mark

Immediately use Cross Slash to activate the mark. This triggers its associated effects and creates Shocked Ground through Charged Mark.

Shock causes the affected enemy to take 20% increased damage. Applying it at the beginning of your rotation therefore increases the damage dealt by your subsequent attacks.

Because of Eternal Mark, the target remains marked after the initial activation. You retain the benefits of Predator's Mark while taking advantage of the mark's triggered effects.

### Step 3: Build Freeze with Lunar Assault

Use Lunar Assault to build up Freeze on the target. Continue until the enemy is primed for Freeze, which occurs at 70% Freeze buildup.

Watch the target's health bar or the blue bubble above its head to recognize when it is ready.

### Step 4: Freeze the Target with Arctic Howl

Once the target is primed for Freeze, use Arctic Howl. This freezes the enemy and grants you empowered attacks.

At this point, the target is frozen, Shocked, and still marked, allowing you to take advantage of your damage bonuses.

### Step 5: Use Shred to Generate Ice Fragments

While the target remains frozen, attack with Shred. Each hit generates ice fragments, which accumulate on the ground and will be detonated by Cross Slash.

Your goal is to fit as many complete Shred combos as possible into the Freeze duration. Depending on your attack speed and the duration of Freeze, you may be able to perform one or two complete combos. Deep Freeze support on Arctic Howl can help extend the available window.

You will need to learn the timing through practice, as the number of attacks you can fit depends on your particular setup.

### Step 6: Finish with Cross Slash

Before the target unfreezes, use Cross Slash again. This gathers and detonates the accumulated ice fragments, delivering their damage in a single burst.

Because Cross Slash is supported by Biting Frost, it also consumes the target's Freeze to increase its own damage. You are therefore combining the damage from the accumulated fragments with a stronger finishing hit.

Once your character is sufficiently geared, this complete sequence can deal enough damage to eliminate bosses in a single combo.

## Mapping and General Clearing

You don't need to perform the full rotation against every enemy. The setup is designed to maximize single-target damage, especially against bosses, while ordinary mapping gives you much more freedom.

Keep Lunar Blessing active, use Pounce to move around the area, and use Lunar Assault to clear enemies. You can incorporate Cross Slash whenever it is useful, but there is no need to execute every step of the boss rotation against every pack.

The Werewolf's movement speed and mobility make it particularly effective when you keep moving through the map rather than remaining stationary to attack.

## Core Spirit Gems

Several Spirit Gems are central to the Werewolf's gameplay. Some are used across every Werewolf build, while others depend on the character's defenses, mana requirements, and available skill slots.

### ![Herald of Ice icon](/images/werewolf-skills/herald-of-ice.webp) Herald of Ice

Herald of Ice is used by every Werewolf build. When a frozen enemy dies, it explodes, allowing the damage to chain through groups of enemies and making it an important part of your mapping setup.

### ![Eternal Rage icon](/images/werewolf-skills/eternal-rage.webp) Eternal Rage

Eternal Rage is also used by every Werewolf build. Its purpose is to help you maintain Lunar Blessing by providing the Rage needed to reactivate it when its cooldown becomes available.

Lunar Blessing consumes all your current Rage when activated, so you want to recover enough Rage during its duration to activate it again as soon as possible.

In the late game, you can move Eternal Rage to an Absent Amulet to avoid reserving 100 Spirit for it. This becomes an option once your character is sufficiently geared.

### ![Berserk icon](/images/werewolf-skills/berserk.webp) Berserk

Berserk is commonly used because it increases the benefits gained from Rage. Since Werewolf builds rely on Rage for damage and may also gain other benefits from it, Berserk can improve several aspects of the build at once.

Not every build uses Berserk, but it is an important option to consider when planning your Spirit Gem setup.

### Maintaining Lunar Blessing

To maximize Lunar Blessing uptime, you should always socket cooldown reduction and prolonged duration supports into the skill.

These supports work alongside Eternal Rage. Prolonged duration makes the buff last longer, cooldown reduction allows you to reactivate it sooner, and Eternal Rage helps ensure that you have enough Rage available when the cooldown ends.

Combining these three elements allows you to maintain Lunar Blessing almost continuously, and with a suitable setup, you can keep it active at all times.

### ![Savage Fury icon](/images/werewolf-skills/savage-fury.webp) Savage Fury

Savage Fury is another Spirit Gem used frequently, although it is not universal. It can be placed in your second weapon set so that it does not consume your active Spirit reservation while still charging up.

When activated, Savage Fury grants Onslaught and additional damage.

Whether you can fit it into your setup depends partly on your available skill slots.

### ![Mana Remnants icon](/images/werewolf-skills/mana-remnants.webp) Mana Remnants

Mana Remnants is an option for builds that need help sustaining their mana. Werewolf builds can have substantial mana requirements, and Mana Remnants can help address this when you have room for it.

It is particularly useful for builds that rely on critical hits, since most Werewolf builds invest in critical strikes, although not all of them do.

Mana Remnants is not mandatory. If you cannot fit it into your setup, you will need to solve your mana problems through other means.

### Defensive Spirit Gems

Werewolf builds deal substantial damage, but defenses are often one of their biggest challenges. You should therefore use your available Spirit reservation to address the defensive needs of your character whenever possible.

For evasion-based characters, Wind Dancer is a common choice. For Druids with hybrid Energy Shield and Armour defenses, Barkskin is an option.

The appropriate defensive Spirit Gem depends on the character and the rest of the build. These are examples rather than universal requirements.

## Managing Skill Slots, Mana, and Attributes

Werewolf builds can be demanding in terms of mana, attributes, and skill slots. These limitations are important to consider when putting together your character.

By default, you have nine skill slots, and a Werewolf build can easily fill all of them. You need to accommodate your main skills and Spirit Gems, so you may not have enough room for every useful option.

This is one reason why Savage Fury and Mana Remnants are not always included, even when you meet their requirements or would benefit from their effects.

An Unset Ring can provide an additional slot, giving you room for another skill or Spirit Gem. Additional slots can be particularly valuable once your setup becomes more demanding.

You should also account for the Werewolf's high attribute requirements and mana consumption when planning your gear and passive tree.

## Passive Tree and Lineage Support Priorities

### Heavy Frost

Heavy Frost is a passive-tree node that every Werewolf build should aim to acquire. It makes your damage against frozen enemies behave as though those enemies have no resistances.

This is particularly important because Lunar Assault and Shred are your two primary damage abilities. Lunar Assault builds Freeze, while Shred deals damage during the Freeze window. Heavy Frost allows Shred to take advantage of the frozen target without being held back by its resistances.

### Rakiata's Flow

Rakiata's Flow is a powerful Lineage Support Gem that causes supported hits to treat enemy elemental resistances as inverted. Since Werewolf builds deal substantial elemental damage, this is a major late-game upgrade.

Rakiata's Flow is expensive and difficult to obtain, so you should treat it as a chase item rather than something you can expect to have early in your progression.

The support only applies to the skill it is socketed into. For Werewolf builds, that skill is Lunar Assault.

Before you acquire Rakiata's Flow, you should invest in elemental penetration on the passive tree so that Lunar Assault can deal damage effectively against enemies with elemental resistances.

Once you obtain Rakiata's Flow, you can remove those elemental penetration investments and allocate the points elsewhere, whether toward additional damage or defenses.

The reason this works is that Lunar Assault is responsible for building Freeze, while Shred takes advantage of the frozen target. With Heavy Frost, your damage against frozen enemies behaves as though they have no resistances. Rakiata's Flow therefore addresses the resistance problem for Lunar Assault, while Heavy Frost covers Shred during the Freeze window.

Your passive-tree priorities will change significantly once you obtain this support. It is worth planning for both stages of progression rather than building around an item you do not yet have.

## Defense Through Movement

One of the most important defensive habits for a Werewolf is to keep moving.

Your high movement speed and mobility allow you to reposition quickly and dodge incoming attacks. You should treat this mobility as part of your defensive setup rather than simply a way to move between enemies.

This is particularly important while mapping. When surrounded by large groups of monsters, avoid standing in one place and attacking continuously. Attack, move, attack, move, and keep repositioning as you clear the area.

Against a single boss, standing in place to execute your damage rotation is often fine when the situation allows it. During general mapping, however, remaining stationary for too long exposes you to unnecessary danger.

Werewolf builds can deal excellent damage, but their defenses often require more attention. Using your movement effectively helps compensate for that weakness.

## Leveling as a Werewolf

You can level your character as a Werewolf from the beginning, and it is a viable option if you enjoy the playstyle. However, depending on your ascendancy, leveling entirely as a Werewolf may be less efficient because several of your most important abilities unlock at different stages of progression.

There are two major power spikes to keep in mind.

**Level 22: Cross Slash.** Cross Slash gives you access to an important part of your damage sequence by allowing you to detonate accumulated ice fragments. This is a substantial improvement to your damage.

**Level 52: Lunar Blessing.** Lunar Blessing is another major power spike because it increases your cold damage and adds moonbeams to your melee attacks. A significant portion of the Werewolf's power comes from this ability, so the build becomes much more effective once you have access to it.

Before level 52, the Werewolf can feel considerably weaker for some players, particularly when the ascendancy does not provide enough to compensate for the missing ability.

You have two main options. You can level as a Werewolf throughout the campaign if you enjoy the playstyle, or you can level using a different setup and transition into Werewolf at level 22 or level 52.

The best timing depends on your ascendancy and how you want to approach leveling. If you want to use the Werewolf as early as possible, you can do it from level 1, but level 22 is an important milestone. If you prefer to wait until the core setup is available, level 52 is the other major transition point.
