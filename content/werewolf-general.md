## Introduction {#intro}

This guide covers the mechanics shared by all werewolf builds in Path of Exile 2. The individual ascendancy guides cover the choices specific to each character. They build on these shared mechanics and explain how each character can adapt them to its particular strengths and requirements.

### How the Werewolf Plays

The werewolf has several skills that work together as a combo. You don’t need to use all of them all the time, but you should have every one slotted with a hotkey and use them at the right moments. There is a clean single target rotation that gives you the best damage, and pressing your buttons regularly gets you both damage and survivability.

That makes for a very active playstyle. Path of Exile players usually don’t like pressing many buttons, but the werewolf skills follow each other so naturally that the rotation feels fluid, smooth, and fast. It never feels like a burden.

The werewolf is fast in both movement speed and attack speed. It covers a lot of terrain and dodges enemy attacks well. Your movement is one of your biggest assets, and you should use it as a tool for both offense and defense.

### Talisman Forms

You play the werewolf by equipping a talisman and selecting a form in it. There are three forms: bear, werewolf, and wyvern. Each one gives a passive bonus. The werewolf form gives movement speed and nothing else.

## The Core Skills {#skills}

### ![Shred icon](/images/werewolf-skills/shred.webp) **Shred**

**Shred** is your basic attack. You get it by equipping a talisman and selecting the werewolf form, so it comes with the form by default. Because it is a natural ability that unlocks levels as you level up, you can’t apply Jeweller’s Orbs to it. Its support gem slots open as follows. You get the first two slots naturally when you unlock the ability, the third unlocks at level 40, the fourth at level 65, and the last at level 90, which is when all of its slots are available.

**Shred** is a combo attack. You hit three times, and the fourth hit is a bigger, slower one that hits harder. Those three hits plus the big one make one full **Shred** combo, and I’ll use that term from here on. When you **Shred** a frozen target, you drop ice fragments on the ground, and they detonate after a short delay for some damage.

### ![Lunar Assault icon](/images/werewolf-skills/lunar-assault.webp) **Lunar Assault**

**Lunar Assault** is the first skill you get when cutting gems. Its damage is modest. You use it to freeze targets, because it applies a lot of freeze buildup.

### ![Pounce icon](/images/werewolf-skills/pounce.webp) **Pounce**

**Pounce** is a jump, and a very good one. Other builds use it too. It helps you cross the field quickly, and since you are already running fast as a werewolf, you can sprint and dodge on top of it. It also helps when you need to go uphill or downhill and there is no open path.

The jump has a second part. Whatever you hit when you land gets marked with **Predator’s Mark**. A marked target takes more damage depending on the number of enemies around it. If you trigger the mark, you summon a wolf, a normal wolf that acts as a minion around you. At some point you will have several wolves, so you mark the target with **Pounce** and it takes more damage from you because of all the wolves around it.

### ![Arctic Howl icon](/images/werewolf-skills/arctic-howl.webp) **Arctic Howl**

**Arctic Howl** is a war cry. A target is primed for freeze when it sits at 70% or more freeze buildup. If your howl hits a primed target, it freezes that target automatically. If your howl hits a primed target or one that is already frozen, you also get empowered attacks for your next few attacks. This is why it pays to prime a target with **Lunar Assault** and then freeze it with **Arctic Howl**. You save the time it would take to freeze it with **Lunar Assault** alone, and you get the empowered attacks at the same time.

### ![Cross Slash icon](/images/werewolf-skills/cross-slash.webp) **Cross Slash**

**Cross Slash** deals damage in an area in front of you, and it works best as a finisher. When **Shred** hits a frozen target, it leaves ice fragments on the ground. **Cross Slash** gathers all of those fragments into the spot where you slash and detonates them instantly. That is a lot of damage at once, so think of it as an execution. **Cross Slash** also detonates a mark when it hits a marked target.

### ![Lunar Blessing icon](/images/werewolf-skills/lunar-blessing.webp) **Lunar Blessing**

**Lunar Blessing** turns you into the white werewolf. You unlock it at level 52. You gain increased cold damage, and your melee skills start raining moonbeams on the monsters around you. The moonbeams hit for a lot of damage. Keeping **Lunar Blessing** up for as long as you can is the main goal, since it is where a large share of your damage comes from. It is the skill that enables the werewolf.

### Support Gems on the Core Skills

On **Pounce** we use **Eternal Mark**. Normally, detonating a mark removes it. With **Eternal Mark**, the target stays marked after you detonate it, so it keeps taking the extra damage from **Predator’s Mark**.

We also put **Charged Mark** on **Pounce**. When you detonate a mark, it leaves shocked ground, which shocks the enemy. A shocked enemy takes 20% increased damage, so your following hits all deal more.

On **Cross Slash** we use **Biting Frost**. It consumes the freeze on the target to hit for more damage. That is another reason to use **Cross Slash** on a frozen target. You get the ice fragment detonation, and the slash itself hits harder because it consumes the freeze.

On **Arctic Howl** you can slot **Deep Freeze**, which increases how long the target stays frozen.

On **Lunar Blessing** we always use two supports. One is **Cooldown Recovery** and the other is **Prolonged Duration**. The reason is covered in the spirit gem section below.

## The Rotation {#rotation}

### The Single Target Rotation

This is the full rotation for dealing the most damage to a single target. Start by making sure you are transformed into the white werewolf, so activate **Lunar Blessing** before the fight and enter combat with it already running.

**Pounce** > CS > **Lunar Assault** > **Arctic Howl** > **Shred** > CS

**Pounce** the target to mark it, then immediately **Cross Slash** to detonate the mark. Detonating the mark gives you benefits that depend on the mark you are using, including the shocked ground from **Charged Mark**. Thanks to **Eternal Mark**, the target stays marked, so it keeps taking the extra **Predator’s Mark** damage.

Next, use **Lunar Assault** on the target to build freeze buildup. You can tell the target is primed for freeze in two ways. You can see its freeze buildup reach 70% on its health bar, and a little blue bubble floats above its head. Once it is primed, use **Arctic Howl** to freeze it.

After that, stop using **Lunar Assault** and switch to **Shred**. While the target is frozen, you shred and drop ice fragments on the ground. How many full **Shred** combos you can fit depends on your attack speed and on how long the freeze lasts, and you will only get a feel for it by playing. It will be one full combo or two. Fit whatever the freeze allows.

Before the target unfreezes, finish with **Cross Slash**. It gathers all the ice fragments and detonates them at once, and since **Biting Frost** consumes the freeze, the slash itself hits harder as well. In the endgame, once you are geared, this full combo deletes any boss in the game, because all that damage lands at once.

### Mapping

When clearing maps you don’t need to run the full rotation. In most cases you can **Lunar Assault** things and they explode. If you want more variety, you can **Pounce** and **Cross Slash** as well. Stay in **Lunar Blessing** as much as possible. Mapping gives you a lot of freedom, but the basic loop is **Lunar Blessing** up, **Pounce** around the map, **Lunar Assault**, and destroy everything.

Don’t stand still while mapping. Single target boss fights are fine to stand in place for, but with huge packs around you, you should attack and move, attack and move, over and over. Use your speed, your dodge, and your mobility as a defensive layer. It would be better if the game’s balance didn’t demand this much engagement, but it does, and moving will make you more successful even if it gets annoying.

## Spirit Gems Used Across Werewolf Builds {#spirit-gems}

These are not part of the rotation, but almost every werewolf build uses them, with only a few exceptions.

### ![Herald of Ice icon](/images/werewolf-skills/herald-of-ice.webp) **Herald of Ice**

**Herald of Ice** is used by every werewolf build. When a frozen target dies, it explodes, which creates a chain of damage for clearing maps.

### ![Eternal Rage icon](/images/werewolf-skills/eternal-rage.webp) **Eternal Rage**

**Eternal Rage** is also in every werewolf build. It matters because we want **Lunar Blessing** up for as long as we possibly can. **Lunar Blessing** lasts longer the more rage you have. When you press it, it consumes all of your rage and extends its duration based on how much you had. We want to be at full rage again by the time the cooldown comes back. **Eternal Rage** is the only comfortable way to do that. Combined with **Cooldown Recovery** and **Prolonged Duration** on **Lunar Blessing**, it gives us a long **Lunar Blessing** whose cooldown is ready when it wears off, so we have it up at all times. Depending on the character, the difference might be one second, but it adds up. Late in the game, once you are geared, **Eternal Rage** moves onto the **Absent Amulet**, because we don’t want to spend 100 spirit on it.

### ![Berserk icon](/images/werewolf-skills/berserk.webp) **Berserk**

**Berserk** is in almost every build, though not all of them. It amplifies the benefits you get from rage. All of our builds use rage, and we even take maximum rage to increase how much we have. Depending on the build, rage gives us benefits beyond damage, and **Berserk** amplifies all of them.

### Build-Dependent Spirit Gems

Other spirit gems show up often but depend on the build. **Savage Fury** goes in the second weapon set. That way it doesn’t consume spirit, but it still charges up. When you activate it, you get onslaught and a lot of extra damage. **Mana Remnants** helps with mana problems in some builds, and it works better in builds made around critical strikes. Most werewolf builds go critical, but not all of them.

The last common category is a defensive layer that fits the character. On evasion characters we use **Wind Dancer**. On the druid, shaman, and oracle, which are hybrid energy shield and armour, I use **Barkskin**. The werewolf is very good at damage and struggles most with defenses, so I cover that with spirit gems whenever I can.

### Mana, Attributes, and Skill Slots

The werewolf is hungry for three things. It needs a lot of mana, a lot of attributes, and a lot of skill slots. You only have nine skill slots by default, and the werewolf tends to fill every one of them. Sometimes you can’t fit **Savage Fury** even though it would cost no spirit, because there is no slot left. The same goes for **Mana Remnants**, and then you have to solve your mana another way. When you run out of slots, you might pick up an **Unset Ring**, because it gives you a new slot for another skill or spirit gem.

## Resistances, Rakiata’s Flow, and Heavy Frost {#rakiatas-flow}

Every werewolf build wants the **Rakiata’s Flow** lineage support gem at some point. It inverts the enemy’s resistances when you attack them. Since we deal a lot of elemental damage, inverted resistances mean extra damage. It is an endgame item and very expensive, so it is a chase item for us. Every werewolf build uses it, and there is a clear before and after.

Before you have **Rakiata’s Flow**, you want elemental penetration on your passive tree so that **Lunar Assault** penetrates resistances. After you get it, you can remove all the elemental penetration from the tree and spend those points on other damage or on defenses, since you no longer need penetration.

**Rakiata’s Flow** only supports one skill, which will be **Lunar Assault**. That works out because our two main damage skills are **Lunar Assault** and **Shred**. **Lunar Assault** freezes the target with inverted resistances. For **Shred**, every werewolf build takes the **Heavy Frost** node on the passive tree. With it, any damage you deal to a frozen enemy counts as if the enemy had no resistances. So you freeze with **Lunar Assault**, and then **Shred** hits a target with no resistances. Both skills are covered.

## Leveling as a Werewolf {#leveling}

You can level as a werewolf. I do it all the time. It isn’t very efficient with some ascendancies, though, because the werewolf needs all of its skills to work properly, especially **Lunar Blessing**, and they unlock at different levels. There are two big power spikes while leveling. At level 22 you can get **Cross Slash**, and at level 52 you can get **Lunar Blessing**. For some builds, the werewolf will feel weak before level 52. **Cross Slash** is big damage and **Lunar Blessing** is where most of your power comes from, so another option is to level as something else and swap to the werewolf at level 22 or at level 52, depending on what you want.
